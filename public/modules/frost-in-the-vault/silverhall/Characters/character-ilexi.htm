Ilexi Tinctwhistle

Gnome Alchemist (Grenadier)

“Reagent, reagent, little spark — tell me what you hide in the dark.”

Background

Ilexi Tinctwhistle grew up in Silverhall’s artisan district, blowing glass and distilling everything she could get her hands on. Recruited to work in a Lebeda laboratory at a young age, she pioneered techniques for mineral assays and arcano-chemical bonding. When the first tainted coin crumbled, her detection compound turned bright green — and the Hollow Chill mystery truly began.

Her invention — the reagent — is now the party’s first line of defense against forged wealth. But Ilexi sees a bigger picture: what if the reagent could not only detect — but undo? Her mind races faster than her hands can follow, but she keeps trying.

Role in the Party

Ilexi is a bomb-thrower, trap disruptor, and chemical genius. She specializes in precise battlefield control, supporting allies with clever consumables while lighting up enemies with targeted alchemical blasts. No one can craft or adapt gear like she can.

Personality

Hilariously hyper-focused and nervously brilliant, Ilexi talks too fast, triple-checks her own work, and keeps her goggles on even indoors. She hoards beakers like a dragon hoards gold. Her hands are always stained. She’ll risk her life for a breakthrough — and has.

Character Hooks

  • Her reagent is the only known tool that can detect Hollow Chill corrosion in processed ore.
  • She suspects the contaminant may be a perversion of one of her earliest acid fusion experiments.
  • She intends to design a “counteragent compound” by campaign’s end — but it will require field testing.

How to Play Ilexi Tinctwhistle

Ilexi is a battlefield control expert with huge flexibility. She disables with bombs, supports with alchemical items, and identifies dangers others miss. Her splash damage is safe around allies thanks to Precise Bombs. She’s ideal for players who love planning ahead, improvising tools, and muttering things like “I brought just the thing.”

  • Open fights with bombs. Use splash weapons to slow or weaken groups — without harming your allies.
  • Deploy consumables. Smoke sticks, tanglefoot bags, and flasks are part of your toolkit — even during downtime.
  • Identify threats. Craft (Alchemy) and Knowledge (Nature) will often detect Hollow Chill traces before they spread.
  • Stay mobile. You’re best at mid-range — let your positioning dictate your impact.
