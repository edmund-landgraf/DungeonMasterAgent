Sava of Zmeyka

Half-Elf Rogue (Scout)

“No one sees me until they regret it.”

Background

Sava grew up in the back alleys of Zmeyka, ferrying packages along trade routes no taxman dared cross. She learned which footsteps meant trouble, how to speak three truths and one lie, and how to vanish in the middle of a crowd.

When a client sold her out to Surtovan enforcers, Sava disappeared for real. She re-emerged months later, working under a shadow contract with House Lebeda — a rogue with no fixed address and a long memory.

Role in the Party

Sava is the party’s scout, flank-striker, and skill monkey. She moves fast, hits hard from stealth, and disarms threats before they’re noticed. Her rogue talents make her essential in cities, tunnels, and trap-laden ruins.

Personality

Quiet but not shy, Sava watches more than she speaks. She values loyalty — the kind earned through pain. Her humor is dry, her steps silent, and her blade never far from someone’s shadow.

Character Hooks

  • Still haunted by who betrayed her in Zmeyka — and the whispered rumor that they now work in Port Ice.
  • Her **Veil of the Eel**, taken from a drowned agent, may link her to a larger network of forgotten spies.
  • She doesn’t trust nobles — even the ones who pay her. Especially the ones who pay her.

How to Play Sava

Sava is a high-Dex, stealth-focused rogue who shines when flanking or ambushing. Use terrain, surprise rounds, and movement to deliver sneak attacks, then vanish again.

  • Scout ahead. High Stealth and Perception make her an ideal front-runner.
  • Use Sneak Attack. Flank with Serune or Lazlo for consistent extra damage.
  • Trap handling expert. Disable Device and Trap Spotter trigger automatically.
  • Escape and re-engage. Her AC, dodge item, and mobility keep her alive when surrounded.
