Karzak Deepstem

Dwarf Druid (Cave Druid)

“The mountain does not forget. And neither do I.”

Background

Born deep beneath the Golushkin Mountains, Karzak grew up among dwarves who listened to stone and spoke with earth spirits. After surviving a collapse that killed half his clan, he devoted his life to keeping the balance beneath the world. Now, the mountains speak of poison in the gold veins, and Karzak knows it's time to return to the surface.

Role in the Party

Karzak is the shield — a front-line defender and spiritual anchor. His magic manipulates terrain, slows enemies, and draws power from the deep. His guidance is often quiet but always grounded, especially when the rest of the party feels adrift.

Personality

Karzak is gruff, practical, and deeply rooted in tradition. He trusts stone over speech and action over promises. When he speaks, his words are few and firm. When he acts, he does so with unwavering certainty. He rarely smiles, but never abandons a companion.

Character Hooks

  • The Golushkin stone spirits warned him of a forgemaster “injecting false fire into the earth.”
  • He once buried a corrupted druid alive in Highdelve. That grave has been disturbed.
  • He is searching for a lost animal companion — a blind cave badger — last sensed near Port Ice.

How to Play Karzak Deepstem

Karzak plays like a battlefield tactician and party anchor. He isn't flashy — but he shapes the flow of encounters with control, protection, and endurance.

  • Use terrain-controlling spells like Entangle or Stone Call to hinder groups of foes.
  • Soak early damage. You have great AC and HP for a caster. Stand firm when others must reposition.
  • Protect the natural world. Karzak will not stand for abuse of magic, life, or land — even from allies.
  • Use Stonecunning and Survival outside of combat to track underground threats and identify corrupted veins.
