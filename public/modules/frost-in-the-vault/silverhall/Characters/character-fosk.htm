Fosk

Cave Badger Companion (Karzak’s Bonded Ally)

“The mountain never forgets.”

Background

Fosk was lost during a reconnaissance expedition in the mines beneath Highdelve — or so Karzak believed. In truth, the badger was trapped after a Hollow Chill channel burst through a fault line. Karzak now carries the guilt of abandoning his companion, and Fosk bears a pale streak of frost down one flank — a mark that reacts to Hollow Chill presence.

Companion Role

Fosk supports Karzak in combat and exploration. His keen senses, burrowing ability, and loyalty make him a perfect fit for the cave-born druid. In combat, he trips and flanks. In downtime, he watches with unmoving vigilance.

How to Use Fosk

  • Use him to scout narrow tunnels and sealed passages with Scent and Stonecunning.
  • He can trip enemies or defend spellcasters in tight spaces.
  • Trigger his Cave Rage when Karzak is harmed, turning him into a serious threat.
  • His resistance to cold-tainted brine makes him an early-warning system against Hollow Chill exposure.

Companion Stat Block

Type: Animal (Small)
Hit Dice: 3d8+6 (HP: 21)
Armor Class: 17 (10 base + 3 Dex + 2 natural + 2 barding)
Speed: 20 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 10 ft.
Attack: Bite +4 (1d6+1), 2 Claws +2 (1d3+1)
Abilities: STR 13, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 2, WIS 12, CHA 4
Saves: Fort +5, Ref +6, Will +2
Special Abilities:
• Darkvision 60 ft.
• Scent
• Stonecunning (+2 Perception on stonework)
• Cave Rage (1/day): When Karzak drops below half HP or is struck within 30 ft., Fosk gains +2 STR and CON, –2 AC for 3 rounds.
• Cold Sense (Passive): Fosk growls within 20 ft. of brine-infected ore or active Hollow Chill taint.
Tricks Known:
Attack, Defend, Come, Track
(Additional tricks may be learned with Handle Animal)
