Serune Quen

Dhampir Fighter (Armiger)

“Steel doesn’t compromise. Neither do I.”

Background

Serune Quen once enforced the laws of Port Ice as an elite arm of Surtovan discipline — a black-armored sentinel known for swift justice and colder mercy. But when ordered to carry out a purge on a harmless coastal commune, she refused. Declared a traitor, she was forced to flee the city she had sworn to defend.

Now in Silverhall, she fights not for law or titles — but for control, stability, and those who still believe in structure. Her halberd is her voice, her stance a command. Her shield, when drawn, is the law made manifest.

Role in the Party

Serune is a frontline tank who can adapt her strategy based on the battlefield. With her halberd, she controls space and punishes aggression. With her sword and shield, she becomes a true wall — protecting allies and holding choke points with unshakable defense.

Personality

Stoic, disciplined, and grimly principled. Serune trusts few, but protects those she does with fierce resolve. She values order over peace, and sees compromise as the first step toward failure. Her fearsome calm can silence a room.

Character Hooks

  • Exiled from Port Ice after disobeying a purge order; blacklisted by Surtova.
  • Wields three ceremonial weapons of black steel with magical flames — part of a “weapon pack” forged by unknown parties.
  • Each weapon’s flame is symbolic only — their true potential remains locked, tied to her fate in Port Ice.

How to Play Serune Quen

Serune is a flexible frontliner. Use her halberd for wide-area control and attacks of opportunity, or switch to sword and shield for close-quarter defense and team protection. Evaluate each battle's terrain and needs.

  • Halberd Mode: Threaten zones with 10-foot reach and punish movement.
  • Shield Mode: Boost your AC and defend adjacent allies with Bodyguard and Shield Wall-style feats.
  • Use Combat Reflexes to make multiple attacks of opportunity.
  • Trigger Stand Still or Trip when enemies move through your space.
  • Protect squishier allies by forming shield lines and absorbing blows.
