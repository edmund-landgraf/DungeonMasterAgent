Serune Quen – Fighter (Armiger, Mythic Build)

“Steel doesn’t compromise. Neither do I.”

Overview

Serune is the party’s tank and tactical controller — locking down foes with polearm reach or defending allies with shield-line discipline. Her mythic path emphasizes control, attacks of opportunity, and unbreakable defense. Built for the front line, she turns narrow corridors and chokepoints into enemy graves.

Class Progression: Fighter (Armiger) 12 — consistent feat access, armor mastery, and weapon flexibility with halberd/sword+shield setups.

Mythic Tier Progression – Guardian Path

  • Tier 1: Mythic Power, Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Defensive Move
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Mythic Combat Reflexes
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Power Attack
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Ever Ready — bonus to AoOs and attack/damage
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Improved Trip

Key Tactics

  • Halberd Setup: Control reach with Stand Still + AoOs
  • Trip Chain: Greater Trip → Dazzling Display → Cornugon Smash → Demoralize
  • Shield Mode: Use Sword + Shield with Shield Focus when defending VIPs or casters
  • AoO Stack: Mythic Combat Reflexes + Ever Ready = massive threat zone

Playstyle Tips

  • Hold narrow terrain and punish movement with trip + Stand Still
  • Switch to sword + shield when protecting weakened party members
  • Use Cornugon Smash to intimidate spellcasters before they act
  • Let casters bait foes into your reach for control counterattacks
