Sava of Zmeyka – Rogue (Scout, Mythic Build)

“No one sees me until they regret it.”

Overview

Sava is a stealth-focused scout and skirmisher. Her mythic path sharpens her burst mobility, initiative dominance, and sneak attack damage. Built for ambushes, infiltration, and high-value target takedown, Sava thrives in trap-laden ruins and corrupt urban sprawls alike.

Class Progression: Rogue (Scout) 12 — full sneak attack scaling, trap sense, and scout’s charge synergy with Mythic movement boosts.

Level-by-Level Progression

  • Level 1: Rogue 1 — Sneak Attack +1d6, Trapfinding, Trap Spotter, Weapon Finesse
  • Level 2: Rogue 2 — Evasion, Rogue Talent: Combat Trick → Dodge
  • Level 3: Rogue 3 — Sneak Attack +2d6; Feat: Two-Weapon Fighting
  • Level 4: Rogue 4 — Rogue Talent: Fast Stealth; +1 DEX
  • Level 5: Rogue 5 — Sneak Attack +3d6, Uncanny Dodge; Feat: Weapon Focus (Shortsword)
  • Level 6: Rogue 6 — Rogue Talent: Finesse Rogue (DEX to damage)
  • Level 7: Rogue 7 — Sneak Attack +4d6; Feat: Bleeding Attack
  • Level 8: Rogue 8 — Rogue Talent: Pressure Points; +1 DEX
  • Level 9: Rogue 9 — Improved Uncanny Dodge; Feat: Improved Two-Weapon Fighting
  • Level 10: Rogue 10 — Sneak Attack +5d6; Rogue Talent: Combat Trick → Mobility
  • Level 11: Rogue 11 — Feat: Improved Initiative
  • Level 12: Rogue 12 — Sneak Attack +6d6; Rogue Talent: Offensive Defense; +1 DEX

Mythic Tier Progression – Trickster Path

  • Tier 1: Mythic Power, Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Surprise Strike
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Mythic Sneak Attack
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Two-Weapon Fighting
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Precision Critical
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Weapon Focus (Shortsword)
  • Tier 6: Path Ability: Shadow Stealth

Key Talents

  • Scout’s Charge: Enables Sneak Attack on a charge (10+ feet)
  • Fast Stealth: Move at full speed while hidden
  • Pressure Points: Sneak damage causes debuffs
  • Offensive Defense: Gain AC when sneak attacking

Playstyle Tips

  • Lead with Surprise Strike and flanking charges
  • Use Bleeding Attack to soften heavy targets
  • Escape or reposition with Shadow Stealth
  • Scout and disarm traps ahead of the party
