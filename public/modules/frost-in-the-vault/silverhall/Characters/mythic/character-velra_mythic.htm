Frost in the Vaults – Mythic Character Progression

Velra Wynne – Inquisitor (Abadar, Mythic Build)

“Let justice be as cold as the coin — and as sharp.”

Overview

Velra’s mythic path enhances her core strengths: truth-seeking, tactical leadership, and divine retribution. She relies on judgment, spellcasting, and teamwork to dismantle corruption — whether political or profane.

Class Progression: Inquisitor 12 — full spellcasting and judgment access outweighs any multiclass option.

Level-by-Level Progression

  • Level 1: Inquisitor 1 — Judgment, Touch of Law, Stern Gaze, Monster Lore
  • Level 2: Inquisitor 2 — Cunning Initiative, 1st-level spells
  • Level 3: Inquisitor 3 — Solo Tactics; Feat: Precise Strike
  • Level 4: Inquisitor 4 — Second daily Judgment; 2nd-level spells
  • Level 5: Inquisitor 5 — Bane weapon enhancement; Feat: Spell Penetration
  • Level 6: Inquisitor 6 — Teamwork Feat: Shielded Caster
  • Level 7: Inquisitor 7 — 3rd-level spells; Feat: Iron Will
  • Level 8: Inquisitor 8 — Discern Lies (3 rounds/day)
  • Level 9: Inquisitor 9 — Feat: Step Up
  • Level 10: Inquisitor 10 — Second Bane/day, 4th-level spells
  • Level 11: Inquisitor 11 — Teamwork Feat: Seize the Moment
  • Level 12: Inquisitor 12 — Judgment use/day increase; full divine toolkit unlocked

Mythic Tier Progression – Hero Path

  • Tier 1: Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Inspired Spell
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Mythic Judgment (stack multiple bonuses)
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Spell Penetration
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Unwavering Judgment — judgments cannot be suppressed
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Iron Will
  • Tier 6: Path Ability: Mythic Bane — adds +2d6 damage and +2 enhancement

Spell Highlights

  • Low-level: Shield of Faith, Divine Favor, Command
  • Mid-level: Hold Person, Zone of Truth, Invisibility Purge
  • High-level: Discern Lies, Righteous Might, Flame Strike

Playstyle Tips

  • Use Judgment for survivability or precision — adapt mid-fight as needed
  • Combine Bane with Mythic powers for deadly single-target burst
  • Control battlefield truth: Discern Lies, Zone of Truth, and Sense Motive
  • Position to share Teamwork Feats with Serune and Lazlo
  • Use the Coin of Mercy for post-battle healing support
