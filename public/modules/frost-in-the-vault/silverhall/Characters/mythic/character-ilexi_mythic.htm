Frost in the Vaults – Mythic Character Progression

Ilexi Tinctwhistle – Alchemist (Grenadier, Mythic Build)

“Reagent, reagent, little spark — tell me what you hide in the dark.”

Overview

Ilexi is a bomb-throwing genius, battlefield controller, and alchemical prodigy. Her mythic path enhances her explosive versatility, control capabilities, and survivability in tight encounters. She thrives in trap-filled dungeons, chemical puzzles, and Hollow Chill research crises.

Class Progression: Alchemist 12 (Grenadier) — to maximize bomb power, discoveries, extract utility, and field chemistry.

Level-by-Level Progression

  • Level 1: Bombs (Precise Bombs), Throw Anything, Mutagen
  • Level 2: Discovery: Infusion (share extracts)
  • Level 3: Feat: Point-Blank Shot
  • Level 4: Discovery: Smoke Bomb; +1 INT
  • Level 5: Feat: Precise Shot; 2nd-level extracts
  • Level 6: Discovery: Stink Bomb
  • Level 7: Feat: Weapon Focus (Bombs)
  • Level 8: Discovery: Tanglefoot Bomb; +1 INT
  • Level 9: Feat: Extra Bombs; 3rd-level extracts
  • Level 10: Discovery: Force Bomb
  • Level 11: Feat: Quick Draw
  • Level 12: Discovery: Explosive Bomb; +1 INT

Mythic Tier Progression – Trickster Path

  • Tier 1: Mythic Power, Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Alchemical Savant
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Mythic Bomb (+2d6, bypass DR)
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Point-Blank Shot
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Precision Bombing
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Weapon Focus (Bombs)
  • Tier 6: Path Ability: Detonation Trick

Key Discoveries & Tactics

  • Precise Bombs: Never hit allies with splash
  • Infusion: Give key extracts like Shield or Invisibility to allies
  • Smoke/Stink/Tanglefoot Bombs: Control battlefield movement and vision
  • Force Bomb: Destroys incorporeal or DR-heavy foes
  • Explosive Bomb: Area denial and burst finishers

Playstyle Tips

  • Use Quick Draw and Grenadier’s swift draws to attack every round
  • Prepare extracts before battle: Expeditious Retreat, Barkskin, Resist Energy
  • Leverage Detonation Trick for ambush setups, chokepoints, or retreat bombs
  • Don’t forget Disable Device and Knowledge (Nature) for Hollow Chill detection!
