Frost in the Vaults – Mythic Character Progression

Karzak Deepstem – Cave Druid (Mythic Build)

“The earth does not rush, and neither do I. But when it moves… nothing stands.”

Overview

Karzak is built to withstand the horrors of cold corruption, sabotage, and hostile terrain. His progression favors defense, terrain control, and summoning. Mythic tiers are selected for battlefield resilience, divine utility, and theme alignment with the Hollow Chill arc.

Class Progression: Druid 12 (Cave Druid). Multiclassing would reduce Karzak’s spellcasting, elemental forms, and companion strength.

Level-by-Level Progression

  • Level 1: Druid (Cave Domain); Feat: Toughness
  • Level 2: Wild Empathy; Animal Companion: Cave Badger (Fosk returns!)
  • Level 3: Feat: Spell Focus (Conjuration)
  • Level 4: Wild Shape 1/day (Small Elemental); +1 WIS
  • Level 5: Feat: Natural Spell — enables casting while Wild Shaped
  • Level 6: Wild Shape 2/day
  • Level 7: Feat: Craft Wondrous Item — create anti-corruption charms
  • Level 8: +1 WIS; Wild Shape 3/day
  • Level 9: Feat: Augment Summoning — boost Earth/Cave allies
  • Level 10: Wild Shape (Large Elementals); 5th-level spells
  • Level 11: Feat: Iron Will — reinforce Will save
  • Level 12: +1 WIS; Access to 6th-level spells like Stone Tell and Repel Wood

Mythic Tier Progression – Hero Path

  • Tier 1: Mythic Power, Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Enduring Armor
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Mythic Wild Shape (Swift action activation, gain DR)
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Spell Focus (Conjuration)
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Earth Resistance (ignore stone terrain penalties, resist acid)
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Toughness
  • Tier 6: Path Ability: Mythic Summons (summoned elementals gain DR, temp HP)

Animal Companion

Fosk, Karzak’s cave badger, scales with Druid level. Primary roles: flanking, tremorsense, terrain burrowing. At higher levels, Fosk gains Evasion, Devotion, Multiattack, and excellent saves.

Spell Highlights

  • Low-level: Entangle, Stone Call, Obscuring Mist, Barkskin
  • Mid-level: Spike Growth, Call Lightning, Air Walk, Wall of Thorns
  • High-level: Wall of Stone, Stoneskin, Repel Wood, Earthquake, Stone Tell

Playstyle Tips

  • Shape terrain and hinder enemies with battlefield control spells
  • Use Wild Shape into elementals for reach, flight, or mobility
  • Summon Earth-themed allies enhanced by Augment + Mythic feats
  • Resist Cold and Acid frequently; cast Barkskin before fights
