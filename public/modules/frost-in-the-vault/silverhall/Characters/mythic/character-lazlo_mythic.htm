Frost in the Vaults – Mythic Character Progression

Lazlo – Magus (Frostbound, Mythic Build)

“Cold is the truest form of control. Everything slows, even time.”

Overview

Lazlo is the party’s arcane striker and crowd freezer. As a Frostbound Magus, he blends devastating spellstrikes with slowing effects, elemental control, and mythic enhancements to mobility and precision. His icy magic is a perfect thematic counter to the Hollow Chill plot.

Class Progression: Magus (Frostbound) 12 — to maximize spell combat, cold damage synergy, and arcane versatility.

Level-by-Level Progression

  • Level 1: Magus 1 — Spell Combat, Arcane Pool, Frostbite prepared
  • Level 2: Spellstrike, Arcane Pool +1, Weapon Bond: Cold Iron Scimitar
  • Level 3: Feat: Intensified Spell
  • Level 4: Magus Arcana: Frostbite Familiarity; +1 INT
  • Level 5: Feat: Weapon Focus (Scimitar)
  • Level 6: Magus Arcana: Flamboyant Arcana (for extra panache-like mobility)
  • Level 7: Feat: Chilling Spell
  • Level 8: Magus Arcana: Hasted Assault; +1 INT
  • Level 9: Feat: Spell Penetration
  • Level 10: Magus Arcana: Empowered Magic
  • Level 11: Feat: Improved Initiative
  • Level 12: Magus Arcana: Preserve Essence; +1 INT

Mythic Tier Progression – Archmage Path

  • Tier 1: Mythic Power, Surge (1d6); Path Ability: Mythic Spellstrike
  • Tier 2: Path Ability: Endless Frost — cold spells ignore resistances
  • Tier 3: Feat: Mythic Intensified Spell
  • Tier 4: Path Ability: Arcane Surge — cast + move as swift action
  • Tier 5: Feat: Mythic Weapon Focus (Scimitar)
  • Tier 6: Path Ability: Mythic Haste — personal version active longer or re-castable

Key Spells

  • Cold Focus: Frostbite, Snowball, Icy Prison, Cone of Cold
  • Mobility: Mirror Image, Blur, Haste, Dimension Door
  • Utility: Dispel Magic, See Invisibility, Greater Invisibility

Playstyle Tips

  • Engage with Spell Combat + Frostbite for debuff + damage
  • Use Arcane Pool to enhance weapon with frost, keen, or speed
  • Cast Haste + Spellstrike early, then control space with Cone of Cold
  • Preserve Essence helps avoid losing spells to attacks or disruptions
  • Mythic Spellstrike and Arcane Surge let you blitz casters and end fights quickly
