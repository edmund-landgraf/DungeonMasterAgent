Fosk the Pale-Touched

Mythic Animal Companion Progression

Mythic Origin

Fosk, Karzak's loyal cave badger companion, was exposed to lingering energies of Hollow Chill during the events of The Buried Heart. These energies awakened a dormant spark within him, unlocking mythic potential that manifests in both ferocity and resilience.

Mythic Rank 1: Frozen Fury

  • Pale Fang (Ex): Fosk's bite counts as magical and cold iron for the purposes of overcoming DR. Deals +1d4 cold damage.
  • Icevein Awareness (Su): Fosk can detect the direction of Hollow Chill magic within 300 ft., even through solid objects. (Standard action to focus.)
  • Burrow Blitz (Mythic Trick, 1/day): Fosk may burrow and erupt beneath a target, automatically threatening a trip attempt with a +2 mythic bonus to the roll.
  • Mythic Toughness: Gains +5 hit points and becomes immune to fatigue from cold terrain effects.

Mythic Rank 2: Glacial Armor

  • Glacial Armor (Ex): Fosk’s fur grows a crust of magical ice, granting +2 natural armor bonus and DR 2/magic.

Mythic Rank 3: Rime Howl

  • Rime Howl (Su, 1/day): Fosk unleashes a chilling roar in a 30 ft. cone. Creatures take 3d6 cold damage (Reflex DC 14 half) and are entangled for 1 round on a failed save.

Mythic Rank 4: Tectonic Charge

  • Tectonic Charge (Ex): Fosk may burst through solid ground or rocky terrain with no movement penalty. If he passes through enemy squares, he may attempt a bull rush with +4 bonus to knock them aside.

Role in the Party

Fosk continues to serve as Karzak’s sturdy vanguard, but now with mythic-enhanced cold synergy. He is particularly effective against creatures vulnerable to cold or reliant on terrain-based defense.

Visual Cues

  • Frost clings to Fosk’s fur in arcing sigils.
  • Eyes glow faintly in dark or when Hollow Chill energy is near.
  • His breath frosts the air even in warm environments.
