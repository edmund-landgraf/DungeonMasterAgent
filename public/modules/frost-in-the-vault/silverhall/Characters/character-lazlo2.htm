Lazlo Oerlen

Half-Elf Magus (Frostbound)

“Cold doesn’t lie. When it bites, it means it.”

Background

Lazlo Oerlen was once a mercenary spellblade assigned to escort mineral surveyors along Brevoy’s northern borders. During a routine scouting mission near Port Ice, his team triggered an arcane explosion in a collapsed smelter — a surge of elemental cold burst outward, scarring his nerves and altering the flow of magic through his blood. He recovered, changed. His magic runs colder now. Sharper. Unyielding.

He left Port Ice after that — too many questions, too few answers. Since then, Lazlo has traveled under House Lebeda’s quiet employ as a bodyguard, investigator, and arcane consultant. He knows Silverhall well... but doesn’t realize yet how deep the frost runs.

Backstory Extension

Lazlo's cold affinity is more than coincidence — it’s the lingering mark of an ancient planar rift. His exposure at Port Ice left his soul partially aligned with the Plane of Cold. The explosion not only imbued him with power, it awakened dormant energies that now react to the Hollow Chill’s presence. Lazlo is drawn toward cold sources, sensing their magic instinctively. He dreams of glacial voids and voices in the ice.

His powers are evolving. As he levels up, Lazlo gains increasing resistance to cold, a sixth sense for magical frost, and eventually the ability to project planar frostwaves that disrupt enemy magic and movement. His story will culminate in discovering a frozen planar vault hidden near Zmeyka or Grayhaven, a remnant of the same ancient force that awakened his powers.

Role in the Party

Lazlo is a mid-range striker who combines martial weaponry and arcane chill. He steps in when spellcasters falter and swordsmen stumble. With his cold-themed touch spells and battlefield tactics, he’s a perfect hybrid for surgical magical assaults.

Personality

Detached but not unkind, Lazlo is slow to speak and quicker to act. He believes in observation, logic, and holding power in reserve. While his demeanor is cold, he’s deeply loyal — and his dry wit tends to surface when the odds are bleakest.

Character Hooks

  • Lazlo’s spellbook crackles faintly near certain vaults in Silverhall — a behavior he’s never observed before.
  • Unknown to him, his body registers Hollow Chill traces as ambient magical static — but the effect is subtle.
  • He has dreams of the Port Ice smelter fire — dreams that leave frost on his pillow.
  • He may soon feel drawn toward a glacial vault in Zmeyka, where his magic began and where it may one day end.

Scaling Abilities

  • Level 1: Cold Resilience (trait) – +1 save vs cold effects and ice terrain.
  • Level 3: Cold Resistance 5; Spellcraft checks detect magical cold auras intuitively (+2 bonus).
  • Level 5: Once per day, may unleash a Cold Pulse (15 ft cone, 1d6/2 levels cold damage, Ref DC 15 half).
  • Level 7: Cold Resistance increases to 10; immune to naturally occurring cold environments.
  • Level 9: Frost Tracer – May sense the location of any Hollow Chill-infused item within 60 ft.
  • Level 11: Plane of Cold Affinity – gain +2 to caster level checks vs fire effects, and all cold spells ignore resistance.

How to Play Lazlo Oerlen

Lazlo is a “magic-through-blade” character. You’ll alternate between melee strikes and touch-range spells — especially cold-themed ones. You’re tougher than a wizard, but still tactical. Hit fast, then reposition. Use your frost abilities for status effects, not just damage.

  • Cast through your weapon. Use Spell Combat to deliver touch spells like Frostbite.
  • Balance offense and defense. Use Shield or Expeditious Retreat when you need space.
  • Lean into cold status effects. Fatigue, entangle, or slow foes with precise hits.
  • Pair with the party tank. You’re perfect as a flanker or frontline spellstriker.
