Velra Wynne

Tiefling Inquisitor (Abadar)

“Let justice be as cold as the coin — and as sharp.”

Background

Velra was born in the border town of Cazrel, raised in the shadows of courthouses and gallows. Her infernal heritage branded her early, but it was Abadar’s clerics who gave her purpose. Trained as an inquisitor, she pursued corruption like a bloodhound — and never once apologized for her methods.

Role in the Party

Velra serves as the group’s moral compass and legal sledgehammer. Her divine magic exposes lies, her judgment silences the wicked, and her presence alone is often enough to make smugglers rethink their choices.

Personality

Cold and composed, Velra keeps people at arm’s length. She believes in structure, contracts, and coin — and fears how easily trust can be melted. Her wit is dry, her gaze unblinking, and her loyalty hard-earned but unshakable.

Character Hooks

  • Has suspicions that the Church of Abadar may be unknowingly compromised.
  • Received a sealed letter from House Lebeda requesting her aid in Silverhall, citing old favors owed to the church.
  • Fears her tiefling lineage may become a political tool used against the party.
  • Wears the Coin of Mercy, a church-gifted amulet that heals the wounded 3/day — a rare gift granted for acts of restraint.

How to Play Velra Wynne

Velra is a law-aligned enforcer of divine justice — quiet, discerning, and decisive. She excels at identifying lies, punishing corruption, and leading from behind with calm authority.

  • Spot lies and threats first. Use Sense Motive and Perception regularly.
  • Activate Judgment for damage, protection, or accuracy in fights.
  • Focus on chaotic enemies. Your Lawbringer trait gives you an edge.
  • Lead with confidence, not force. Let actions and insights speak louder than shouts.
  • Support the group tactically. Debuff foes, reveal secrets, and turn social pressure into progress.
