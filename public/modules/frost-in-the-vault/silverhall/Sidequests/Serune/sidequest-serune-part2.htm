Encounters – Tomb of Glass Chains

Overview

This document provides a list of combat and trap encounters for Serune Quen's personal quest, Part 2: The Tomb of Glass Chains. Each is designed for a party of level 7–8 adventurers and tailored to the tomb’s magical flame-and-ice environment and legacy undead themes.

1. Undead Miners (CR 5)

Location: Forgotten Garess mine shaft

  • Creatures: 6x Skeletal Miners
  • HP: 22 each
  • AC: 15
  • Attacks: Pickaxe +6 (1d8+2), Rusted tools may cause tetanus (Fort DC 12)
  • Special: Half-action collapse: 1/encounter, one miner may pull a rotting support beam to cause a ceiling partial fall (Reflex DC 15 or 2d6 bludgeoning)

2. Shard-Kin Crypt Guardians (CR 6 – Each)

Location: The Four Crypt Arches

  • The Halberd Bearer: Undead Fighter (Dread Knight)
  • HP 65, AC 20, Power Attack cleaves with +8 (2d6+6), flaming halberd burst on crit (1d6 extra)
  • The Shield-Sister: Graveknight Defender
  • HP 72, AC 22, Shield Slam +9 (1d6+4), Reflective Flame (1d4 damage to attackers that miss)
  • The Iron Flame: Skeletal Sorcerer (CR 6)
  • HP 44, AC 16, Casts: Burning Hands (DC 15), Scorching Ray, Dispel Magic
  • The Frost-Blooded Watcher: Ice-Wight Monk
  • HP 58, AC 19, Flurry of Blows +7/+7 (1d8+3), Chill Touch, causes stagger on crit (Fort DC 14)

3. Tombbound Flame-Chains (Trap, CR 5)

Location: Chainbridge Gallery

  • Trigger: Stepping on cracked glass floor
  • Effect: Animated blackfire chains lash in a 10-ft radius
  • Reflex DC 16: Half damage (3d6 fire or cold, depending on flame choice)
  • Disable Device: DC 18 (must be done in sequence across bridge segments)

4. Undead Echo Choir (CR 7)

Location: The Vault of Oaths

  • Creatures: 3x Tomb Sirens (Banshee-like undead, CR 2 each), 1x Echo Warden (CR 5)
  • Tomb Sirens: Wails force Will DC 15 or become frightened for 1 round
  • Echo Warden: Aura of Silence (30 ft), Melee attacks cause 1d6 sonic + deafened (Fort DC 14)
  • Special: If all 3 Sirens are defeated first, Warden explodes in sonic burst on death (4d6, Ref DC 17)

5. Final Encounter – Varkoss the Shardbound (CR 8 / Mythic 1)

Location: Inner Sanctum of the Tomb of Glass Chains

  • Type: Undead Dhampir Graveknight (Mythic)
  • HP: 110, AC: 24
  • Attacks: Halberd +10 (2d6+6, +1d6 cold or fire), Blackfire Burst 1/day (10-ft radius, 4d6)
  • Special Traits:
    • Obsidian Will: Immune to mind-affecting from dhampirs or undead
    • Shardbound Echo: Reform once as incorporeal for 3 rounds after death
    • Mythic Abilities: Hard to Kill, Surge (+1d6 to any d20), Power Attack ignores DR
  • Combat Notes: 3-phase battle with chain-based mechanics; see full quest write-up
