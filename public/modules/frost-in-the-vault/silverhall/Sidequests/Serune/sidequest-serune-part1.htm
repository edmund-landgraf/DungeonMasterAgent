Graves of the Blood-Forsaken

Part I of II – Serune Quen's Personal Quest

Level Range: 2–3
Location: Northeast of Silverhall, edge of the Gronzi Forest
Theme: Undead resurgence, ancestral burden, the first awakening of the Black Shard Relics

I. Background & Setup

Two centuries ago, a dhampir enclave known as the Black Shard Covenant was allowed to settle in a forested glade northeast of Silverhall after helping to eliminate a vampire cult in the region. Their small chapel, the Sanctuary of Black Mercy, served as both temple and training ground for elite undead-slaying warriors who swore oaths against undeath and tyranny. But betrayal from within led to their downfall. A priestess of Pharasma turned on the order, cursing the site and turning the fallen into guardians of their own crypts. The land was sealed, and its memory faded — until now.

Serune Quen, born generations later, has long carried the shattered relics of the Covenant without understanding their origin. A surge of nightmares, whispers in a forgotten tongue, and a rising sense of dread leads her and her companions to investigate.

II. Act 1 – The Village of Elmridge

This small, reclusive village stands near the Gronzi Forest. Recently, villagers have reported livestock drained of blood, ghastly howling at night, and fog that won’t lift. Tensions run high.

  • Gathering Rumors: Diplomacy/Intimidate/Sense Motive DC 12–14. Reveals strange lights and tales of a ‘fire-eyed knight’ in the woods.
  • Key NPCs:
    • Old Marra – A dying herbalist who recognizes Serune from a vision. She gifts a broken obsidian pendant (matching Serune’s armor seam).
    • Kellis, a scholar-priest – Knows of the Sanctuary through ancient texts. He offers a partial map fragment and confirms the relic’s likely origins.
  • Haunting Graveyard: At night, one grave resonates with Serune’s presence. Blood sigil glows faintly.
    Perception DC 13 (find it), Knowledge Religion DC 15 (identify Black Shard marking).

III. Act 2 – The Withered Trail

The trail to the Sanctuary winds through twisted forest paths shrouded in unnatural mist. The terrain itself seems to resist passage. Echoes follow the party. Cold sap drips from ash trees, and the wind carries voices in an unfamiliar cadence.

  • Environmental Hazards:
    • Blood Glyph Traps – Perception DC 15, Disable Device DC 17. Deal 1d4 negative energy + shaken (Will DC 13).
    • Haunting Fog – Will DC 12 or gain fatigued for 10 minutes. Serune receives flashes of ancestral memory if she fails.
  • Combat Encounters:
    • Fog Wolves (x3) – CR 1/3, concealment advantage in fog, coordinated attacks.
    • Corpse Crows Swarm (x1) – CR 1/4, Distraction DC 11 Fort, flyby swipes (1d4).
    • Gore-Stitched Deer – CR 1, stitched body, charges for 2d6+2, Frightful Moan (Will DC 13).
  • Flavor: An old sword tree bears a Black Shard insignia carved into its bark, causing Serune’s relics to resonate briefly.

IV. Act 3 – Sanctuary of Black Mercy

The Sanctuary is a moss-covered stone structure partially sunken into the forest floor. Its interior is a mix of ruined chapel and blood-cursed crypt. Most of the doors and halls are defiled but intact enough to navigate.

Notable Areas:

  • Desecrated Courtyard: Pharasma’s statue weeps black ichor. Channel Positive Energy (DC 15) cleanses it and grants Serune +1 sacred bonus to saves for 1 hour.
  • Hall of Vigil: Contains broken obsidian shields, burned armor, a banner of the Covenant. Perception DC 14, Knowledge History DC 13.
  • Reliquary Vault: A mirror fragment displays a flashback vision of Vexaria, Serune’s ancestor, giving birth while armor burns beside her.
  • Crypt Gate: Protected by necromantic ward. Spellcraft DC 16, or opens only to dhampir. Failsafe triggers 1d6 negative energy burst (Will DC 13 half).

V. Final Encounter – Mother Elgatha

Once a priestess of Pharasma, Elgatha betrayed the Covenant and became a cursed ghoul oracle, bound to the chapel by the souls she failed. She views Serune as a heretic and a threat to the curse’s integrity.

  • CR 3 Dread Ghoul Oracle (of Bones or Shadow)
  • HP: 40, AC: 16
  • Abilities:
    • Touch of the Grave: +5 melee touch, 1d8+2 negative energy
    • Paralyzing Claw: Fort DC 13, 1d4+1 rounds
    • Spells: Cause Fear, Obscuring Mist, Inflict Light Wounds
    • Covenant Curse: Reanimates once if slain by non-dhampir unless burned or channeled
  • Tactics: Begins in fog, summons ghoul midwives, targets Serune first

VI. Conclusion

After Elgatha’s defeat, the crypt calms. The oppressive energy lifts. Serune is drawn to the sealed reliquary altar, where her blood activates the Black Shard's bond.

  • Relic Unlock: Serune chooses one of her three relics to gain its Level 3 abilities.
  • Codex Fragment: Found in a rusted scroll case — mentions Vexaria and a "shard-bound tomb beneath Garess stone."
  • Village Reaction: Elmridge locals are split — some reverent, others fearful. An old child gifts Serune a carved figure that glows faintly when near her relics.
