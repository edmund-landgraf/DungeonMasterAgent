Serune Quen: Origins of the Black Flame

Prologue: A Child of Forgotten Blood

In the year 4700 AR, during the final frost of spring, a caravan master traveling northeast of Silverhall discovered a strange scene deep in a collapsed ravine: a solitary baby, quiet and unharmed, wrapped in ancient silk and nestled between fragments of obsidian-black armor. The pieces pulsed faintly with unnatural cold, whispering of oaths and betrayal.

The child was taken to a remote shrine where she was named Serune — her name a half-remembered word spoken by one of the caravan guards in a dream the night before. She grew swiftly, unnaturally calm, and her presence unsettled the devout. No one could touch the relics for long without becoming chilled or disoriented — except her.

Legacy Bound: The Black Shard Fragments

The fragments were later identified by a scholar-priest of Abadar as remnants of the Black Shard Covenant, an elite dhampir order thought extinct. Their armor and weapons were forged from Skelverite, a magically reactive volcanic ore mined in the Golushkin Mountains, under House Garess' domain. The Covenant was betrayed by their benefactors and entombed in a secret crypt beneath the mountains.

Each piece of the shattered relics responded to Serune as she aged, reshaping themselves in secret as she trained in war. By adulthood, she bore a full suit of matte-black plate, a jagged obsidian shield, and a glaive whose edge shimmered with heatless flame.

Rumors of Lineage and Fate

Among scattered tomes and encoded codices, her tutors uncovered the name Vexaria, a dhampir noble-knight who vanished shortly before the fall of the Covenant. Vexaria was known to have been pregnant and stationed at a forward chapel near Silverhall called the Sanctuary of Black Mercy. Some believe Serune is her last blood — others suspect darker origins.

Her dreams are filled with snow-choked gates, chains forged in flame, and the voice of a gaunt knight whispering, “The flame chooses.”

Political Whispers and Dangerous Interest

House Garess, long weakened after the Vanishing of House Rogarvia, has gone silent on the topic of the Covenant’s existence. A Garess archivist once hinted that the betrayal of the Covenant was carried out to prevent the revelation of dangerous truths — secrets that may connect to the Rogarvian disappearance itself.

Meanwhile, House Surtova has begun to show quiet interest in Serune's activities. Agents observe her movements. In court, rumors whisper that her armor may hold the key to a vault that could undo the current balance of power in Brevoy.

The Fire Awakens

Now at the dawn of her adventuring life, Serune travels with her companions not only as a blade and bulwark — but as a living legacy. Her relics, once inert, have begun to burn with either searing flame or killing frost, changing with her will. Each quest brings her closer to uncovering the truth buried beneath Garess stone — and closer to the final oath that will either redeem or destroy her bloodline.
