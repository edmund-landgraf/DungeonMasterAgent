Black Shard Relics

Overview

The Black Shard Relics are a legacy-bound three-piece set of obsidian equipment tied to Serune Quen's dhampir bloodline. Forged from Skelverite ore by the long-buried Black Shard Covenant, these items grow in power as Serune matures, allowing her to channel magical flame — cold or hot — with devastating and defensive effects.

Black Shard Halberd

  • Level 1: Masterwork halberd (nonmagical).
  • Level 3: +1 magic weapon. 1/day: +1d4 fire or cold damage for 1 min.
  • Level 5: +1d6 flame; crits cause shaken (hot) or slowed (cold) (DC 14 Will).
  • Level 7: +2; gains flaming or frost quality (choose daily).
  • Level 9: Crit burst: 10-ft radius flame pulse, 2d6 dmg (DC 16 Reflex).
  • Level 11: Flame bypasses resistance; counts as aligned weapon.
  • Level 13: Blackfire Aura: 3 rounds, +2d6 damage, immunity to flame type.

Black Shard Shield

  • Level 1: Masterwork tower shield (nonmagical).
  • Level 3: +1 magic shield; +2 Intimidate while wielded.
  • Level 5: Melee attackers take 1d4 fire or cold damage.
  • Level 7: +2; 1/day ranged bash: 2d6 fire or cold (15 ft).
  • Level 9: Grants DR 2/— vs undead and constructs.
  • Level 11: Blackfire Reflection: 50% ranged/spell reduction + 1d6 return flame.
  • Level 13: 1/day Shield Other (reverse) against adjacent undead target.

Black Shard Armor (Full Plate)

  • Level 1: Masterwork full plate (nonmagical).
  • Level 3: +1 armor; +1 Will vs necromancy/mind effects.
  • Level 5: Flame Thread: visual flame at 50% HP, enemies -1 to attacks.
  • Level 7: +2; Resistance 5 to fire or cold (chosen daily).
  • Level 9: Crit trigger: 5-ft pulse of 2d6 flame (once/encounter).
  • Level 11: Resistance 10 to both fire and cold.
  • Level 13: Night Vein Surge: 1/day swift cast; DR 5/magic, +2 AC, +2 saves, flame feedback (1d6) for 3 rounds.

Set Bonus: The Black Shard Covenant Lives

  • Flame effects bypass all undead resistances and immunities.
  • Blackfire Aura, Flame Thread, and Reflection activate together once/day.
  • +4 sacred/profane bonus to Intimidate and Sense Motive while set is worn.
