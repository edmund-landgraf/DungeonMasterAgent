Encounters – Tomb of Glass Chains

Overview

This document provides a list of combat and trap encounters for Serune Quen's personal quest, Part 2: The Tomb of Glass Chains. Each is designed for a party of level 7–8 adventurers and tailored to the tomb’s magical flame-and-ice environment and legacy undead themes.

Act I – Forgotten Garess Mine

The adventure begins with a descent into a long-abandoned Garess mining shaft. Haunted by the first signs of undeath, this area serves as the party’s entry point into the deeper tomb complex.

  • Encounter: 6x Skeletal Miners (CR 5)
  • Hazards: Collapsing beams (Reflex DC 15, 2d6 bludgeoning)
  • Clue: A collapsed tunnel reveals a hidden obsidian glyph glowing faintly in Serune’s presence.

Act II – The Four Crypt Arches

Four interconnected chambers each guard a resting place of a former Shard-Kin warrior. Their restless spirits animate to challenge Serune’s legitimacy.

  • Encounters:
    • The Halberd Bearer – undead fighter with flaming cleaves
    • The Shield-Sister – reflective graveknight defender
    • The Iron Flame – skeletal sorcerer with searing spells
    • The Frost-Blooded Watcher – flurrying ice-wight monk
  • Design: The party may choose the order of the chambers. Completing all four opens the central spiral path downward.

Act III – The Chainbridge Gallery

Suspended over a deep crevasse, the gallery is crossed by a shattered glass bridge held by chains. Magical flame pulses through the air.

  • Trap: Tombbound Flame-Chains (CR 5)
  • Skill Challenge: Acrobatics or Disable Device DC 18 to safely cross without triggering multiple segments
  • Optional Encounter: Blackfire Elemental (use Small Fire Elemental stat block + cold subtype)

Act IV – Vault of Oaths

This echoing ceremonial chamber holds records of ancient Shardbound vows and the memory-locked essence of past initiates. Ghostly voices test the party’s resolve.

  • Encounter: Undead Echo Choir (CR 7)
  • Creatures: 3x Tomb Sirens + 1x Echo Warden
  • Features: Aura of Silence, Sonic burst death effect
  • Reward: A vision of Vexaria’s final vow, granting +2 bonus to saves in the final room

Act V – Inner Sanctum: The Shardbound Throne

The tomb’s heart is a cold obsidian throne chamber surrounded by coiling blackfire chains. There waits Varkoss the Shardbound.

  • Boss Encounter: Varkoss (CR 8 / Mythic Tier 1)
  • Phases:
    • Phase 1: Varkoss tests Serune’s martial prowess
    • Phase 2: Blackfire chains animate — party must disable node crystals
    • Phase 3: Varkoss yields or erupts with final Blackfire if defeated dishonorably
  • Reward: All relics reach mythic level. Obsidian Amulet activates with artifact-tier powers
