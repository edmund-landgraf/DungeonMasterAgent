Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Side Quest

The Buried Heart

Karzak's lost companion claws toward freedom beneath Silverhall's forgotten stone.

Background

Years ago, Karzak Deepstem was working as a geomancer and spiritual overseer for a Lebeda mine west of Silverhall. When a blast rune failed, the tunnel collapsed — burying three dwarves and his loyal badger companion, Fosk. The company sealed the site and moved on. Karzak never truly did.

Quest Trigger

Upon reaching Level 2, the party hears of strange growls and rock-shifting noises in an abandoned shaft near Silverhall. A miner named Malra Greft seeks help, referencing “a god made of dirt with claws like chisels.” Karzak recognizes the site immediately — and suspects the impossible.

Quest Structure

1. Breach the Hollow

The shaft is sealed but breachable through brute force (DC 18), engineering checks, or stone-shaping magic. Inside, an echoing tunnel descends into cracked shale and stale air.

2. Troglodyte Squatters (CR 3–4)

  • Troglodyte Bone-Seer (CR 2)
  • 2x Troglodyte Hunters (CR 1 each)
  • Optional: Diseased cave lizard pet (CR 1/2)

The trogs view Fosk as a sacred beast — or a tool to dig deeper into Silverhall’s forgotten underpaths.

3. Fosk Reunited

If approached with calm or familiar command, Fosk instantly recognizes Karzak. Handle Animal (DC 15) or Speak with Animal can complete the rebonding ritual. He rejoins the party at full HP.

XP Rewards
• 600 XP – Combat encounters
• 200 XP – Roleplay and mine navigation
• 100 XP – Reunion scene
Total: 900 XP (shared)
Quest Rewards
• Karzak regains Fosk (Cave Badger Companion)
• Fosk gains +2 Perception in stone or underground terrain
• Optional: Symbol tied to Zirvokksan cult found among trog belongings

Roleplay Moment

When freed, Fosk barrels into Karzak, whimpering and pressing into his leg. The druid may whisper a dwarven phrase of return: “You remembered the mountain... so the mountain remembered you.”
