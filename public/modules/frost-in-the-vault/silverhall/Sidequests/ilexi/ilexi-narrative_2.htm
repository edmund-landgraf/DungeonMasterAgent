Stelladex Observatory – Narrative Guide

Introduction

The Stelladex Observatory looms half-buried in windswept rock, its ancient brass dome cracked and tarnished, its arcane lenses shattered in the collapse. It was once a fusion of astronomy and planar research, where sages and engineers of Brigh studied constellations to chart the motion of hidden dimensions.

The facility was sealed abruptly during a planar disturbance that fractured the inner vault, and few dare to approach its exposed upper galleries. But now, its engines hum faintly with activity once more, triggered by Ilexi’s presence and the approaching planar alignment foretold in Brigh’s lore.

Entrance Approach

The main doors have long since collapsed. Entry is gained through a fractured lens gallery, now open to the elements. Cold winds moan through broken machinery. Magical residue crackles faintly from shattered pylons. Ilexi feels a pull—this is the right place.

Environmental Flavor

  • Loose pages of astronomical records blow across the floor like fallen leaves.
  • Faint blue light occasionally flickers from broken focusing rings overhead.
  • The sound of a distant ticking device echoes through the vents—though nothing is visibly moving.

Room-by-Room Storytelling

Chamber 1: Entrance Chamber

The walls bear engravings of Brigh’s sacred gear constellation. A pressure plate trap tied to an illusionary glyph pulses with forgotten energy. One of Ilexi’s tools glows in resonance here—the same signature as her quartz component.

Chamber 2: Central Lens Room

This was the heart of the observatory, where light was filtered and refracted into planar sequences. A collapsed balcony reveals a glimmering rift in the lens below—still stable. The rift can cause temporary hallucinations (sight of alternate timelines).

A spiritual echo (a haunt) remains, the emotional memory of an acolyte who died while stabilizing the rift. She mutters equations and points repeatedly at Ilexi. If appeased with a Brigh symbol, she releases her hold with gratitude.

Chamber 3: Archive Hall

This corridor is half-buried. Crawling through reveals documents infused with residual light magic. An arcane wight, formed from a collapsed researcher, guards these notes jealously. It recognizes Ilexi as a successor and targets her unless calmed or destroyed. The wight whispers of the "last scroll" and the failure to align the vault correctly.

Chamber 4: Scroll Vault

The final room is round and sunken, ringed by broken gyroscopic platforms. The scroll floats in stasis above a pedestal. Arcane lenses must be manually rotated and aligned to form the proper pattern—the planetary sigil of Brigh. Each movement changes two rings, and the mechanism emits tones as it rotates. A wrong alignment causes a gentle planar shock.

Ilexi must lead the puzzle resolution. A successful solve unlocks the scroll. Failure can still result in access through force or spellcraft, but the lens shatters and permanently alters the visual effects of the scroll’s casting.

Stargazing Abilities (Non-Combat)

  • Planar Navigation (Knowledge [Planes] or Profession [Astrologer] DC 18): Identify which stars correlate with active planar tides. Success grants a +2 circumstance bonus on the next use of any planar travel spell.
  • Sky Archive (Linguistics or Knowledge [History] DC 15): Decipher constellations that act as a mnemonic archive. Each success yields one ancient observatory record (valuable lore, historical prophecy, or forgotten celestial event).
  • Starclock Calibration (Craft [Alchemy] or Knowledge [Engineering] DC 20): Reactivate a starclock mechanism that can determine the local planar boundary strength. Once per day, this device can detect planar instability (as detect magic, but only for planar energy).
  • Constellation Mapping (Survival or Knowledge [Geography] DC 17): Chart visible constellations from the dome. On success, the party gains +2 on navigation or overland travel checks for the next 24 hours.

Closing Vision

When the scroll is bonded, a soft glow envelops Ilexi’s goggles. The ceiling retracts, revealing stars shifting visibly overhead. A translucent image of the acolyte appears and smiles before fading into a gear of starlight. Ilexi knows her path is true. The prototype is one step closer to completion.

DM Tips

  • Keep encounters short and ambient. This is a story space for reflection and clever problem solving.
  • Let Ilexi take the lead but allow all PCs to assist meaningfully.
  • Use sound, light, and memory as motifs throughout the observatory.
