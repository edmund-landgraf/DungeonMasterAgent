Tinctwhistle's Precision Optics

Item Type

Wondrous Item — Eyes Slot

Description

This intricate pair of steampunk goggles gleams with brass detailing and rotating lenses set in a forged iron frame. Infused with magically attuned quartz and etched with ancient planar inscriptions, these goggles are uniquely bound to Ilexi Tinctwhistle. When worn, they amplify her alchemical precision, battlefield awareness, and stargazing abilities.

Scaling Benefits

Level 3: Activation

  • Bomber’s Eye: As a swift action, Ilexi may activate a magical sight effect 3/day. For 1 minute, she gains +1 to attack rolls with thrown splash weapons and ignores soft cover from allies.
  • Alchemical Clarity: Gains +2 on Craft (Alchemy) checks when identifying potions or crafting bombs.

Level 5: Enhanced Perception

  • Arcane Targeting: Ilexi may reroll one failed bomb attack roll per day.
  • Twilight Vision: Grants low-light vision or darkvision 30 ft (whichever she lacks).

Level 7: Planar Resonance

  • Celestial Filter: 1/day, Ilexi may activate a constellation-based filter, allowing her to see invisible or ethereal creatures for 1 minute.
  • Starbound Recall: Once per day, she can recall a used extract slot of up to 2nd level after a successful Knowledge (Planes) or (Arcana) check (DC 18).

Level 9: Brigh's Insight

  • Clockwork Synchrony: As an immediate action 1/day, gain +4 initiative and act in the surprise round even if unaware of the ambush.
  • Enhanced Splash Radius: All alchemical splash weapons Ilexi uses gain +5 ft radius (must choose before throwing).

Level 11+: Astral Convergence

  • Planar Infusion: Her bombs deal an additional 1d6 force damage (or cold damage if chosen) against outsiders and extraplanar creatures.
  • Stargazer’s Command: Stargazing abilities now grant +4 insight bonuses instead of +2 and can be used twice per day.

Crafting Notes

Materials Required: Magically infused quartz, Stelladex Inscription Scroll, brass mountings, arcane lens set

Creation Requirements: Craft Wondrous Item, bomber’s eye, see invisibility, detect magic

Appearance

The lenses shift color based on activation—orange for explosive targeting, blue for arcane vision, and violet for stargazing alignment.
