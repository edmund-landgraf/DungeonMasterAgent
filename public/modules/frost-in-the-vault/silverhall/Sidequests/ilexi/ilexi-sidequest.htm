Fragments of Clarity – Ilexi's Sidequest

Quest Overview

Character Focus: Ilexi Tinctwhistle
Level: 3
Quest Start: Silverhall
Objective: Recover a magically infused quartz and the Scroll of Opto-Inscription to finalize Ilexi's prototype goggles: Tinctwhistle's Precision Optics.

Terrain Progression

  • Gronzi Forest Floor: Dense brush, spiderweb ravines, mist.
  • Foothills: Shale paths, echoing valleys, unstable ledges.
  • Alpine Ridge: Wind-swept open terrain above the treeline, beneath the snowline.

Gronzi Forest Encounters

Thornkin, Forest Warden (CR 2)

Neutral Fey with entangle, charm, and riddling speech. Can be bypassed with Diplomacy DC 16 or suitable crafted gift (Craft Alchemy DC 14).

Web Ravine (CR 2)

Two Giant Crab Spiders guard a mist-covered ravine. Falling into the web trap triggers 10 ft drop (1d6), entangled condition.

Needlefang Drake (CR 3)

A stealthy predator camouflaged in bramble. Breathes a cone of piercing splinters. Can be distracted with glowing alchemical item (Craft DC 15).

Foothills Encounters

Echo Howlers (2x CR 1)

Hyena-like pack predators with echo-locating tactics. Use high ground and laughter to intimidate.

Cavern Slurk (CR 2)

Toad-like creature hidden in collapsed watch path. Uses mucus slick to create a slippery battlefield.

Minor Earth Elemental (CR 3)

Drawn by the Astral Quartz. Collapses parts of the path to create sliding hazards. Weak to sound damage.

Alpine Ridge Encounters

Alpine Harpies (2x CR 2)

Transparent-feathered singers lure PCs to ledge edges using Will saves (DC 13).

Cold Elemental (Small, CR 3)

Frost aura 5 ft (1 cold/round). Weak to alchemical fire. Slam attacks and chill-based aggression.

Lantern Goat (CR 2)

Bioluminescent, modified with a Brigh-aligned heart. Emits a 1-round blinding pulse. Drops rare alchemical catalyst upon defeat.

Stelladex Observatory

Area 1: Entrance Chamber

  • Residual Glyph Trap: Color Spray, Will DC 13, disable DC 16.
  • Treasure: Planar compass, spellcraft kit, leyline notes.

Area 2: Central Lens Room

  • Haunt: Dimensional Echo (CR 2), Confusion 1 round, Will DC 15. Destroyed with catalyst or Brigh symbol.
  • Hazard: Random beam pulses (Reflex DC 14 for 1d6 force).

Area 3: Collapsed Archive Hall

  • Arcane Wight (CR 3): INT drain, image spells, weakness to [light].
  • Treasure: Crystal Memory Cube (Planar Alignment Log).

Area 4: Scroll Vault

A glowing scroll is suspended in a gyroscopic platform. Four arcane lens platforms must be aligned.

Puzzle – Gyro-Lens Alignment

  • Stepping on a platform rotates it and its opposite 90°.
  • Goal: All show the same planar symbol (Arcana DC 12).
  • Failure: 3 wrong moves trigger a scroll shockwave (1d4 force).
  • Alternate: UMD DC 17 or Disable Device DC 19 to bypass.

Final Reward

  • Scroll of Opto-Inscription: Arcane scroll with planar sigils that binds to goggles. Spellcraft or UMD DC 16 to use correctly.
  • Vision: Upon bonding, Ilexi sees constellations rotating above her. The arcane wight’s voice softly blesses her endeavor.
