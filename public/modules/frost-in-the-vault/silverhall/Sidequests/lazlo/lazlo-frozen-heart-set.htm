The Frozen Heart – Relic Set

Relics of Icebound Power

Overview

Forged through forgotten Coldheart experiments and planar resonance chambers, the Frozen Heart set consists of three relics that awaken only for Lazlo. Each piece grants increasing resistance to cold, elemental augmentation, and metaphysical insight. When all three are collected, Lazlo may unlock his Frozen Warden form.

The Frozen Heart (Unlocked in Part 1)

  • Passive: Cold Resistance 5
  • Once/day: As a swift action, Lazlo can sheath himself in frostlight, granting +1 AC (deflection) and dealing 1d4 cold to melee attackers for 1 round.

Stage 2: Ice Skin (Added Ability)

Lazlo can now form an icy barrier around himself. Once per day, he gains Ice Skin as a swift action:

  • Functions like barkskin: +2 natural armor bonus
  • Absorbs the first 10 points of physical damage from a single source
  • Lasts for 1 minute

The Frozen Hand (Unlocked in Part 2)

  • Passive: +1 cold damage on melee/touch spells
  • Touch of Frost: Once/day, next melee touch deals +3d6 cold; Fort DC 14 or staggered for 1 round
  • Cold Resistance increases to 10

The Frozen Head (Unlocked in Part 3)

  • Passive: +2 bonus on saves vs. cold and mind-affecting effects
  • Frozen Insight: Once/day, Lazlo gains +10 on a Knowledge check and sees a frozen vision of the future

Full Set Bonus – Frozen Warden Form

  • 1/day as a swift action, Lazlo becomes the Frozen Warden for 1 minute:
    • Cold Resistance 15
    • All melee attacks deal +1d6 cold damage
    • Any foe striking Lazlo in melee takes 1d4 cold damage
    • Glacial aura: Allies within 10 ft gain Cold Resistance 5

These relics evolve with Lazlo’s journey and may awaken further power under Mythic influence.
