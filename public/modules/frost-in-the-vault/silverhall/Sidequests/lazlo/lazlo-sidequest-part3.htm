Vault of the Last Glare

Part 3 of Lazlo's Frozen Heart Arc

Overview

Buried beneath the icy ruins of Port Ice lies the final Coldheart vault—abandoned after the arcane catastrophe that created Lazlo’s powers. The Vault of the Last Glare pulses with dormant energy, hiding the final relic fragment: the Frozen Head.

Hook

Lazlo’s dreams intensify. He sees his own reflection with a blank, frost-covered face and hears a voice calling from beneath the frozen mines. A former Coldheart enchanter’s journal (recovered from Zmeyka) speaks of a containment vault that “still hums with the warden’s breath.”

Environment

  • Collapsed mining district layered in magical permafrost
  • Arcane interference, causing minor illusions and frost mirages
  • Subterranean caverns partially flooded and bridged by ice

Encounter 1: Coldheart Residuals

Animated scrap-constructs, leaking planar ice energy, attack intruders.

  • CR 5: 2x Coldheart Residuals (Construct)
  • AC 17 | HP 42 | Immune to cold
  • Frost Pulse (1/round, 10-ft burst, 1d4 cold, Reflex DC 14 half)
  • Slow Slam (1d6+4, target must save Fort DC 13 or staggered 1 round)

Puzzle: Crystal Beam Chamber

The path to the final relic is sealed by mirrored cryo-crystals. Players must use environmental features to reflect and focus beams onto three keystones. Correct beam alignment causes a sonic chime and opens a descending stair.

Encounter 2: The Glareborn Apparition

This is a spectral echo of Lazlo’s soul, twisted by the detonation that created him. It guards the relic chamber and speaks in fragments of Lazlo’s thoughts.

Glareborn Apparition (CR 8)

  • HP 75 | AC 18 (incorporeal)
  • Cold Aura: Adjacent creatures take 1d4 cold/round
  • Frostsight Gaze (Will DC 16 or Confused 1 round)
  • Shatterbolt (Ranged touch, 3d6 cold + Fort DC 15 or blinded 1 round)

Reward: Frozen Head (Final Piece)

The final cryoshard floats within a containment sphere. Lazlo’s presence disrupts the barrier, allowing the relic to join him and awaken the full potential of the Frozen Heart.

  • Frozen Head: +2 on saves vs cold and mind-affecting effects
  • Frozen Insight: Once/day, gain +10 on any Knowledge check and a flash of prophetic vision (GM discretion)
  • Ice Skin: Once/day, Lazlo may sheath himself in magical ice as a standard action. This functions as barkskin (CL equal to Lazlo’s level) and absorbs 10 points of damage before dissipating.

Frozen Heart Set Bonus (Complete)

Once per day, Lazlo may enter the Frozen Warden state (1 minute):

  • Cold Resistance 15
  • Melee attacks deal +1d6 cold damage
  • Attackers striking Lazlo in melee take 1d4 cold damage

Aftermath

The vault cracks open, letting in fresh air and dim light. Lazlo’s memories become clearer—he was the only survivor of a Coldheart overload, preserved by the prototype that became the Frozen Heart. Now, he is its final bearer.

DM Tips

  • Let the Apparition speak in Lazlo’s own voice or reveal hidden truths about his past
  • Reward creative use of the beam puzzle with bonuses or shortcuts
  • Use environmental hazards: falling icicles, freezing water, illusory walls
