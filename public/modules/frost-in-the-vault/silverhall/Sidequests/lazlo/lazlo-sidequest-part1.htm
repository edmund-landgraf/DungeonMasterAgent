The Ice Below the Oaks

Part 1 of Lazlo's Frozen Heart Arc

Overview

Within two days' travel from Silverhall, a half-collapsed Coldheart test site slumbers beneath a forgotten grove of oak and alder. Lazlo experiences visions—a hollow wind and the sound of chimes beneath the earth—guiding him to the site. The ruined chambers hold secrets of his origin and the earliest prototype of a Hollow Chill construct.

Hook

A local woodsman mentions strange lights and icy winds flowing from a sinkhole in the hills west of Silverhall. Lazlo feels inexplicably drawn to investigate. Upon arrival, the party finds a hidden tunnel, long sealed, now broken open from within.

Environment

  • Temperate forest with lingering winter chill
  • Occasional icy patches even in sunlight
  • Metallic hums and faint frost-blue glows inside

Smuggler Subplot

The Wyrmbite Traders recently repurposed the upper chambers as a storage alcove. Their presence has disturbed ancient wards, awakening dormant Coldheart echoes. One smuggler remains, injured and terrified.

  • Tellen Varc: Injured smuggler, can recount sighting a frost-figure and Gerrin’s disappearance.
  • Smuggler Journal: Clues to the Hollow Shardbound’s nature and fearsome presence. (Linguistics DC 15)
  • Shipping Invoice: Implies deliveries intended for Zmeyka—hook for Part 2.

Midpoint: The Barricade

The party finds a hastily fortified section with broken crates and magical residue. A Hollow Chill Remnant lurks nearby if they linger too long.

Hollow Chill Remnant (CR 2)

  • AC 16 | HP 30
  • Immune to Cold, Construct Traits
  • Frostbite Touch: 1 nonlethal cold, Fort DC 12 negates

Final Encounter: Cryoshard Vault

The innermost chamber was a resonance test site. A frozen skeleton still radiates planar energy. When disturbed, it reanimates with Cryoshard force.

Cryoshard Revenant (CR 3)

  • HP 45 | AC 15 | Immune to Cold
  • Special: Cryoshard Slam (1d8+2, 1d6 cold)
  • Chilling Wail: 1/day, DC 13 Will save or shaken for 1 minute

Reward: The Frozen Heart (Stage 1)

Lazlo touches the cryoshard remains, and the cold doesn’t harm him—it welcomes him. A fragment of the Frozen Heart awakens.

  • Gain: Cold Resistance 5
  • Once/day: As a swift action, Lazlo can sheath himself in frostlight, granting +1 AC (deflection) and dealing 1d4 cold to attackers who strike him in melee (1 round)

Aftermath

The cave begins to collapse gently, as if its purpose is fulfilled. The smuggler can be escorted out or left. The invoice pointing to Zmeyka becomes the link to Part 2.

DM Tips

  • Highlight ambient horror—cold fogs, frost-eaten footprints, and icy breath effects.
  • Let Lazlo’s powers react to the Cryoshard echoes—glowing skin, hair moving in invisible winds.
  • Consider offering a hallucination or vision of Port Ice or Lazlo’s past during rest.
