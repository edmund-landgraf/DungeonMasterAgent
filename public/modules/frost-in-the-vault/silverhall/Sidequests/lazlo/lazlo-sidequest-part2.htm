Vault of the Bleeding Ice

Part 2 of Lazlo's Frozen Heart Arc (Level 7)

Overview

Set on the jagged cliffs northeast of Zmeyka, the Vault of the Bleeding Ice was once a sanctum for a forgotten order of elemental sages. It was repurposed decades ago by secret agents of House Surtova to study and seal failed cryo-constructs. Within these glacial tombs lies the Frozen Hand—one of three remnants of a shattered core.

Quest Hook

The smuggler's invoice from Part 1 includes notations matching ancient planar sigils associated with the ruined temple. Locals speak of cursed lights flickering over the cliffs on stormy nights and the echo of glass shattering on the wind.

Exploration

The vault consists of three distinct zones:

  • Outer Ruins: Crumbled foundations and ritual markings in the ice. A glyph-locked door requires matching sigils (Knowledge (Planes) DC 16 or Intelligence check DC 18).
  • Sub-crypts: Broken bridges and collapsed tunnels. Acrobatics DC 15 to cross, or Engineering DC 14 to reinforce.
  • Central Chamber: Frozen sarcophagus holding the energy-sealed Frozen Hand relic. Its ward pulses with residual defense energy.

Puzzle – Ice Sigil Circuit

The sigils found in Part 1 allow the players to recreate a resonance circuit to weaken the vault’s wards. Failure triggers a surge trap (Reflex DC 17, 4d6 cold damage).

Encounters

Scavenger Mercenaries (CR 5 Encounter)

  • 3x Human Thugs (Fighter 3): AC 16, HP 30, +5 to hit, 1d8+3 damage.
  • 1x Frost-Gutted Alchemist (Alchemist 4): AC 17, HP 35, Bomb +6 ranged (2d6+2 cold), DC 15 Reflex half.

These scavengers triggered the Coldheart defense and may be hostile or desperate for help.

Mini-Boss – Frostshard Scourge (CR 6)

  • Construct (Cold subtype), HP 72, AC 18
  • Immune to Cold, Fear, Mind-Affecting, Flanking
  • Shatterstep: Teleport 20 ft as a move action, causes burst of icy shards (DC 15 Reflex or 2d6 cold)
  • Coldblade: +8 melee, 1d10+3 slashing + 1d6 cold

Reward – The Frozen Hand

As Lazlo places his hand on the sarcophagus, the Frozen Hand forms around his dominant hand—ice and energy binding to his will.

  • +1 cold damage on melee or touch spells
  • Touch of Frost: 1/day as swift action, next melee touch deals +3d6 cold, Fort DC 14 or target is staggered for 1 round
  • Cold Resistance increases to 10

Aftermath

The vault partially crumbles. The sigils fade, having served their purpose. Among the frost-etched walls is a mural of a frozen mountain—a clue leading to the third vault beneath Port Ice.

DM Tips

  • Reinforce the atmosphere: freezing mist, faint blue glyphs, crunch of ice underfoot.
  • Let Lazlo feel a surge of power—and fear—as his control of cold magic increases.
  • Allow the scavengers to offer insight or a map fragment hinting at Port Ice’s hidden vault.
