Velra Wynne Sidequest – The Ledger of Ash

Overview

Velra Wynne's personal journey begins in the ruins of an abandoned Abadaran temple near Highdelve. Her mission: uncover the truth behind a sealed vault once used to rewrite judgments and determine whether mercy or zealotry should shape divine law. This quest unfolds across five acts and culminates in the acquisition of her second sacred coin: the Coin of Judgment.

Act I – Ash in the Ledgers

Velra receives a coded ledger referencing a lost temple and the operation "Gilded Eye." She and the party travel to the site.

  • Trap: Collapsed Entryway – Strength DC 17 or Engineering DC 15 to clear. Failure causes a 2d6 bludgeoning collapse (Reflex DC 16).
  • Trap: Gilded Tithe Spike Trap (CR 4) – Hidden pressure plate (Perception DC 18, Disable Device DC 18). Causes 1d4 Wisdom damage + -1 Will saves until cleansed.

Act II – Trial by Gold and Fire

The party confronts two fallen inquisitors, once of Abadar’s flock, now purging those they view as merciful weaklings.

  • Enemies:
    • Inquisitor Varos Talgrim (CR 5) – HP 45, AC 18, Heavy Mace +9. Spells: Hold Person, Divine Favor.
    • Inquisitor Maedra Sinn (CR 5) – HP 40, AC 16, Dagger +8, Bane, Zone of Truth, Shield of Faith.
  • Choice: Maedra offers Velra a pact to burn her Coin of Mercy. Refusal leads to a trap in Act III.

Act III – The Vault of Revision

The party accesses the temple vault where judgments were once rewritten.

  • Trap: Abjurant Fire Brazier (CR 5) – 10-ft fire burst (4d6 fire, Reflex DC 16). Half damage if wearing holy symbol of Abadar.
  • Failure to act quickly causes destruction of documents and damage to magical scrolls in the room.

Act IV – Boss: Sorvel Adrus, the Redacted Judge

A condemned undead Inquisitor returns to "correct mercy" in the faith.

  • Undead Wight Inquisitor (CR 7) – HP 85, AC 20
  • Weapon: Contract-Whip +10 (1d6+2, Wisdom damage on crit, trip, reach)
  • Abilities: Aura of Silence (30 ft), Spells: Command, Hold Person, Discern Lies
  • Special: Disavowal Trial – If Velra used Coin of Mercy within 24 hours, she must Will save DC 18 or suffer -2 to divine caster level checks for 1 hour. Reroll allowed if an ally vouches.

Act V – Conclusion & Rewards

Velra preserves the ledger, defeats Sorvel, and rejects the Iron Doctrine. She earns her second relic.

  • XP Earned: 9,200 XP total (2,300 XP per character in a 4-person party)
  • Loot:
    • +1 Lawful Heavy Mace (1,500 gp)
    • Mithral Chain Shirt (1,100 gp)
    • Scroll Tubes: Discern Lies, Break Enchantment, Zone of Truth (1,500 gp total)
    • 3,000 gp from Church tribunal
  • Church Decree: Velra must sign a magical NDA, preventing her from testifying or revealing the contents of the Ledger of Ash to outsiders. Breaking it results in exile from the church’s legal protection.

🪙 New Relic – Coin of Judgment

  • 1/day: Zone of Truth or Calm Emotions
  • Passive: +2 Sense Motive, +1 Will save vs. compulsion
  • Set Bonus: When worn with the Coin of Mercy, both coins glow near active lies or injustices
