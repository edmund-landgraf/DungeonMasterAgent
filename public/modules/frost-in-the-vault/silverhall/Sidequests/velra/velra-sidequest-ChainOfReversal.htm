Velra Wynne Sidequests – The Ledger of Ash & The Chain of Reversal

Overview

Velra Wynne's journey through divine justice continues across two significant quests: The Ledger of Ash (Level 6–7) and The Chain of Reversal (Level 8). These personal arcs deepen her connection to Abadar, test her resolve, and complete her legendary Coin Triad.

Part 2 – The Chain of Reversal

Now a fully empowered inquisitor, Velra is summoned by the Custodial Magistrates of New Stetven to resolve a mysterious disappearance tied to a vault of condemned evidence beneath the city. The case concerns an ancient magical chain used to reverse unjust verdicts — now gone rogue and weaponized by undead dissenters.

Act I – A Broken Tribunal

  • Velra is shown sealed records linking her own ancestor to a politically suppressed trial.
  • Exploring the Church's subterranean archive reveals the Chain of Reversal has been removed by a masked intruder.
  • Trap: Misfiled Curse (CR 6) – Will DC 17 or confusion (1d4 rounds); can be suppressed with Touch of Law.

Act II – The Whispering Verdict

  • Following the trail leads to a former trial hall now occupied by the Verdictless Host — spirits of those executed without due judgment.
  • Enemies:
    • 2x Chainbound Wraiths (CR 6 each) – Entangle + Constitution drain on hit (Fort DC 16)
    • 1x Spirit Advocate (CR 7) – Casts Greater Command, Unholy Blight, and has Aura of Misrule (Will DC 18 or lose lawful traits)

Act III – Vault of Broken Oaths

  • Velra must reconstruct a shattered oath using clues recovered in Acts I and II.
  • Skill Challenge: Knowledge (Religion, History, Law), Diplomacy, Sense Motive. Total DC 55 across three rolls; success prevents ambush.
  • Trap: Oathfire Ward (CR 7) – 20-ft burst of radiant flame (6d6, Ref DC 17 half)

Act IV – Final Boss: Avol Korren, the Unbound Judge

  • Undead Tiefling Inquisitor (Mythic CR 8) – HP 115, AC 23
  • Weapon: Reversal Chain – reach weapon that casts Greater Dispel Magic on crit
  • Abilities: Rewrites effects of spells; uses Zone of Chaos (20-ft aura of shifting alignment traits)
  • Mythic Power: Surge (1d6), Hard to Kill, Mythic Step Up
  • Combat Twist: If Velra chooses to redeem Avol, a second form as a ghostly lawyer rises for negotiation (Diplomacy DC 22 or fight resumes with incorporeal traits)

Act V – Final Judgment

  • Upon defeating or redeeming Avol, Velra is granted her final relic by a divine arbiter of Abadar.
  • The Coin of Redemption appears as a golden coin wrapped in spectral law text.

🧾 Rewards

  • XP Earned: 10,400 XP (2,600 XP per character)
  • Gold: 4,000 gp direct payment + vault access (scrolls, 2 potions of Righteous Might, 1 Abadaran Bonded Ring)
  • Church Status: Velra gains the title Justiciar of the Inner Tribunal

🪙 Final Relic – Coin of Redemption

  • 1/day: Break Enchantment or Atone (caster level = Velra’s level)
  • Passive: +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks to convert enemies or resolve conflicts peacefully
  • Set Bonus (All 3 Coins):
    • Truth Triad: If all 3 coins are worn, Velra can activate Zone of Truth (20-ft) as a free action 1/day
    • Lawflare: Once per long rest, she may invoke the triad to deal 4d6 radiant damage to all chaotic-aligned foes within 30 ft (Will DC 18 half)
