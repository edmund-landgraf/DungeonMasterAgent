Velra Wynne – Coins of Abadar

Overview

Velra bears three divine relics granted by the Church of Abadar for her service, restraint, and redemption. Each coin scales in power every 4 levels (Levels 1, 5, 9). Combinations of coins offer unique boons, also improving at those same level thresholds. Wearing all three coins unlocks the Truth Triad bonus. Each two-coin combination can only be used once per long rest unless otherwise noted.

🪙 Coin of Mercy

  • Passive:
    Level 5 – +2 bonus on Heal checks
    Level 9 – +2 to saves vs. death effects
  • Active:
    Level 1 – Cure Light Wounds (3/day)
    Level 5 – Cure Moderate Wounds (2/day)
    Level 9 – Breath of Life (1/day)

🪙 Coin of Judgment

  • Passive:
    Level 1 – +1 to attack rolls vs. chaotic-aligned creatures
    Level 9 – Aura of Order (10-ft radius): Allies get +2 vs. compulsion effects
  • Active:
    Level 5 – Smite Chaos 1/day (add CHA to attack, level to damage)

🪙 Coin of Redemption

  • Passive:
    Level 5 – +2 Diplomacy when resolving conflict
  • Active:
    Level 1 – Break Enchantment or Atonement (1/day)
    Level 5 – Calm Emotions (3/day)
    Level 9 – Sanctuary Aura (20-ft, 1/day): Allies gain Sanctuary (Will DC 18) for 1 round

🔗 Two-Coin Combinations (Scale with Level)

Wearing any two of the coins grants an additional benefit, which improves every 4 levels. Each combo can be used once per long rest unless otherwise stated.

Mercy + Judgment

  • Active:
    Level 1 – When healing a lawful ally, grant +1 AC and +1 to hit for 1 round
    Level 5 – +2 AC/+2 to hit for 2 rounds
    Level 9 – +3 AC/+3 to hit for 3 rounds; affects two allies

Mercy + Redemption

  • Passive:
    Level 1 – Allies get +2 Will vs fear in 10 ft
    Level 5 – Fear resistance extends to 20 ft
    Level 9 – Allies gain immunity to shaken/frightened
  • Active:
    Level 1 – Healing suppresses fear/confusion for 1 round
    Level 5 – 2 rounds
    Level 9 – 1 round of immunity

Judgment + Redemption

  • Active:
    Level 1 – 1/day designate a foe: +2 to hit, ignore DR for 1 minute; recover Touch of Law on defeat
    Level 5 – +3 to hit, +2 damage, ignore resistances, restore 2 uses
    Level 9 – 2/day designate two foes, also grant +2 saves vs that target

✨ Truth Triad – Full Coin Set Bonus (Scales with Level)

  • Passive:
    Level 1 – Allies gain +1 Will and Diplomacy
    Level 5 – +2 saves vs charm/compulsion
    Level 9 – Allies gain immunity to charm while in aura
  • Active:
    Level 1 – 1/day: Zone of Truth (20-ft radius, free action)
    Level 5 – Add Lawflare (1/day): radiant burst (4d6, Will DC 18 half) vs chaotic foes
    Level 9 – Lawflare becomes 6d6 and dazes on fail (1 round)
