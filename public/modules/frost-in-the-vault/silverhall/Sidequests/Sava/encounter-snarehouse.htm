Frost in the Vaults: Snarehouse Encounter

Encounter: The Snarehouse Ambush

Location: Flooded Smuggler Warehouse ("The Snarehouse") outside Zmeyka
Level: APL 3
XP: 4,000 total (split across enemies)
Objective: Rescue Tolen, an enslaved bard held by the Crooked Net

Encounter Overview

A brine-warped warehouse hides a grim secret — the Crooked Net gang is using the Snarehouse to imprison and traffic fey-touched individuals. Shulla Netwife, a local witch and gang lieutenant, commands the site with summoned creatures, ambushers in the rafters, and magical traps. The structure is flooded in sections, filled with vertical space, poor visibility, and layered hazards.

DM Note: Run this encounter as a multi-phase conflict: scouting, trap management, then escalating reinforcements. Shulla has escape contingencies if cornered. This is a deadly encounter for APL 3 unless the players approach tactically.

Encounter Map & Setup

  • West: Tolen's rusted cage, magically trapped (arcane glyph)
  • Center: Crates, brine channels, moisture-rotted beams, and overhead walkways
  • East: Open brine pool (30-ft cone of water) and escape tunnel partially submerged
  • Above: Skree's sniper perch in the rafters
  • Below: Trapdoor used by Runk the Slab and Small Water Elemental

Enemies & Reinforcements

  • Shulla Netwife (Witch 4): Augmented Summoning (2 Water Mephits), Slumber/Misfortune Hexes, Hold Person, Planar Fold escape spell
  • Jagg Dusthook (Fighter 3): Net axe brawler, holds position near Tolen’s cage
  • Kelp Brothers x2 (Fighter 2/Rogue 1): Initial melee guards, low Will saves
  • Rigslick (Familiar): Delivers hexes, perched near ceiling
  • Water Mephits x2: Breath weapon (1/day), DR 5/magic, immune to fire, vulnerable to cold
  • Small Water Elemental: Initially submerged; emerges in Round 8 to block escape
  • Skree (Human Rogue 2): Concealed sniper with poison bolts (Fort DC 13)
  • Runk the Slab (Half-Orc Fighter 3): Emerges from trapdoor in Round 3–4 with spiked chain, reach + trip

Magical & Mundane Traps

  • Arcane Lock Glyph: DC 20 Perception to detect, DC 18 Disable Device
  • Spore Jars: Fort DC 14 or Sickened (1d4 rounds)
  • Shock Net Launcher: Concealed net that deals 1d4 electric, entangles on fail
  • Collapse Trigger: Shulla can break supports (Reflex DC 15 or fall prone/damaged)

Combat Timeline (for DM Use)

Rounds 1–2:

  • Shulla begins summoning Water Mephits (Augmented Summoning active)
  • Color Spray or stealth can quickly disable 1–2 guards
  • Trap triggers and glyphs begin slowing infiltration

Round 3:

  • Skree opens fire from rafters (poison bolts), Runk enters from trapdoor
  • Shulla casts Obscuring Mist, uses Cackle to maintain hex effects

Round 6:

  • Fosk (Karzak’s Cave Badger) joins and engages Skree
  • Shulla moves toward retreat platform and casts final hexes

Round 8:

  • Shulla completes Planar Fold spell to escape
  • Small Water Elemental emerges near escape path and drops a weakened target

Round 9:

  • Skree is defeated by Fosk in rafter melee
  • Runk is slain in melee while blind in fog
  • Remaining Mephits are finished with cold bombs and magic

Enemy Behavior

  • Shulla avoids melee, maintains hexes, and leaves if below 50% HP
  • Water Mephits prefer aerial harassment and breath cone disruption
  • Runk and Jagg use brute force to lock the central arena
  • Skree targets casters first from the shadows with poisons
  • Small Water Elemental targets fleeing or isolated foes
DM Note: If the party delays or splits focus, the combination of DR, fog, and hexes can lead to a TPK. Use Shulla’s escape as a pivot for future encounters. If captured, Runk or Skree can reveal info about the Coldheart Injectors or the Crooked Net's connections to House Surtova.

Encounter End & Outcomes

  • Tolen: Fey-blooded bard, cursed voice; will not fight but can collapse from fear or sing under compulsion
  • Shulla: Escapes unless interrupted during Round 8 cast (interrupt DC 15)
  • Skree & Runk: If captured, either will attempt to lie or flee if unguarded
  • XP: 4,000 base + 500 bonus for trap disarms, nonlethal captures, or fey insight scenes
DM Note: The runes and brine left behind may hint at deeper connections to Coldheart Injector activity in Highdelve. Tolen’s curse could link to future fey mythos — consider tying his bloodline to river spirits, or Shulla’s former pact with a water-bound entity.
End of Encounter Summary
