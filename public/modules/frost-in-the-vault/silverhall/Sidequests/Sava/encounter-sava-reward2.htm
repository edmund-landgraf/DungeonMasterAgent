Frost in the Vaults: Rewards for Sava

Sava's Story Rewards – After the Snarehouse

Having braved the brine-slick battleground of the Snarehouse and rescued her brother Tolen from slavery, Sava awakens something deeper within herself — the blood of the Gray River, of trickling currents and whispered fey songs. These rewards reflect her personal growth and supernatural lineage.

🌊 Riverborn Echo (Supernatural Trait)

  • Type: Supernatural Trait (story-unlocked; does not count against normal trait limit)
  • Activation: Swift action, 1/day
  • Duration: 1 minute

Effects:

  • +10 ft enhancement bonus to base land speed
  • +2 trait bonus on Acrobatics and Escape Artist checks
  • Gains Evasion for the duration (if not already possessed)
  • While in or adjacent to water, gains +1 insight bonus to AC
DM Note: This trait represents Sava's awakening as river-marked, a trait latent since childhood. Use it to reward fey-themed play or moments of reflection near water.

🎁 Silver Reed Pendant (Wondrous Item – Upgraded)

  • Aura: Moderate conjuration and enchantment
  • Slot: Neck (does not interfere with magical amulets)
  • Craft Requirements: Caster level 5th, summon monster III, heroism, or guidance

Base Effects:

  • +1 resistance bonus on saving throws vs charm and compulsion
  • 1/day: reroll a failed Bluff or Diplomacy check when appealing to someone who reminds her of Tolen

✨ Call the Silver Voice (1/day)

Once per day as a standard action, Sava may manifest the spirit of her brother Tolen through the pendant. His incorporeal form appears as a Skald of half her current level (minimum 1).

  • Type: Incorporeal Spirit (undead traits, but not targetable as undead)
  • HP/AC: As a normal Skald of his level
  • Immunities: Immune to non-magical weapons (standard incorporeal traits)
  • Duration: 1 minute/level or until dismissed/incapacitated
  • Abilities: Has full Skald class features appropriate to level (Inspire Courage, Raging Song, etc.)
  • Customization: Player may custom-build Tolen’s stats; defaults provided if unused
  • Limitations: Cannot use magic items unless spirit-compatible; cannot enter antimagic areas; must stay within visible range

Flavor: This pendant was woven from dried river reeds and silver wire by Tolen as a token of gratitude. Now, bound by Sava’s love and the magic of fate, his spirit answers her call when she needs him most.

DM Note: Tolen may act as a story-based companion in moments of sorrow, victory, or prophecy. His Skald abilities are fully usable during combat. If reduced to 0 HP, he dissipates and may be resummoned the next day fully refreshed.
