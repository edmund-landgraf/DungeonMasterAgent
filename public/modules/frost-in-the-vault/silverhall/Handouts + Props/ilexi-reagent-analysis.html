Ilexi’s Reagent

An investigative compound used to detect Hollow Chill corruption in metallic lattice structures.

Scientific Principle

In metallurgy, cooled metals form a stable crystalline lattice supported by delocalized electron bonding — the “electron sea.” This allows for malleability, strength, and conduction without altering elemental properties. The Hollow Chill process replaces this metallic bonding with a cold-reactive pseudo-lattice which mimics natural structure but collapses under entropy.

Ilexi’s Reagent operates by introducing a trace magnetic excitation and observing its interaction with the lattice. Pure metals remain inert. Tainted ones fluoresce.

Application Process

  1. Apply one drop of reagent to the surface of the item.
  2. Wait 5–10 seconds for activation.
  3. Observe the drop:
    • No reaction – Pure metal.
    • Faint green glow – Hollow Chill suspected.

Reagent Composition

Base: Alkahest suspension
Fluorescent Marker: Shardcap moss extract
Exciter: Lodestone microfilaments
Binder: Cryo-thaumic stabilizing agent

The components create a chemically inert solution until activated by friction or subtle magnetic fields. Cryo-anchored impurities resonate visibly in corrupted material.

“Metal remembers how it cools. If it cooled in deceit, it reacts in fear.”
– Ilexi Tinctwhistle

Limitations

  • Only effective on cooled, forged materials — not ore or molten metal.
  • Reagent deteriorates if exposed to heat or direct light for more than 1 hour.
  • Cannot differentiate origin or timeline of Hollow Chill injection.
  • False negatives possible with magically shielded or warded materials.

Alchemical Category

Type: Diagnostic reagent (external use)
Effect: Lattice phase detection with passive fluorescent output
Craft DC: 23 (Alchemy)
Cost: 45 gp per vial (3 uses)
