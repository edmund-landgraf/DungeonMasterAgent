Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall

Opening Scene – The Crumbling Coin

In the heart of Silverhall, beneath the carved arches of House Lebeda’s counting hall, a mystery begins not with a scream — but a crumble. A gold coin, sealed for years in a temple coffer, collapses into golden dust in Lady Elyra’s gloved hand. This isn’t forgery. It’s sabotage.

Velra Wynne, summoned from the border courts of Brevoy, eyes the powder like ash from a judgment pyre. Karzak Deepstem fingers the base stones of the vault, murmuring to them in Dwarven — and they seem to murmur back. Lazlo Oerlen turns the remains over with a mage's precision, his breath forming frost in the still air. Ilexi, standing on a stool with a satchel of chemical vials at her side, peers through thick lenses and scribbles furiously into her alchemy log.

Sava of Zmeyka watches the group, arms crossed, reading the room like a battlefield — seeing alliances before they’re made. The five are strangers, summoned by a seal of green wax and a single line: “If the coin breaks, so too may Brevoy.”

GM Tip:
Begin with atmospheric tension. Let the coin crumble in silence. Play up sensory detail—dust falling, metal groaning, torches flickering—before any threats appear.

Sample Dialogue

Lady Elyra: “You see this? The seal of Abadar still glows. The coin’s purity was never in doubt... until now.”
Velra: “Purity isn’t a matter of shine. It’s what stays after fire. This failed.”
Ilexi: “It didn’t fail — it was meant to fail. Someone hid decay in the core.”
Karzak: “The stone groans. This metal wasn’t born of the mountain.”
Lazlo: “If this coin were a spell, I’d call it cursed with delay — and timed to collapse a kingdom.”
Sava: “Then let’s find the hammer that struck it — and break the hand that held it.”

Adventure Begins

Their first task is clear: find the origin of the corrupted coin. But every lead points toward someone with a reason to lie. With one foot in the vault and the other in politics, the party must navigate guild halls, temple vaults, and forges that haven’t burned in a decade—or so the records say.

Historical Lore:
Silverhall’s eastern mint once funded half of Brevoy’s standing army. Its forges ran cold during the last political purge and reopened quietly, under Surtovan permission. Now, they may be running again—for the wrong house.

As the frost of the Hollow Chill creeps closer to the city's wealth, five strangers will determine whether Silverhall’s future is forged in truth... or in dust.
