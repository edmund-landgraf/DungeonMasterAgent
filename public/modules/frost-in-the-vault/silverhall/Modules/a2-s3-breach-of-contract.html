Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Act II Encounter

A2-3: Breach of Contract

Infiltration, economic sabotage, and quiet threats beneath the merchant district.

Overview

In Silverhall, where commerce and legality are entwined, coin holds power beyond politics. When one major loan defaults, an old agreement between merchant powers risks igniting a war of ledgers — unless the truth is quietly uncovered.

House Trennel has placed a vault of silver as collateral in a multi-party contract with the influential Goldmark Exchange. After early signs of Hollow Chill were discovered in a third-party delivery, the Exchange suspects something darker lurks in the Trennel reserve. Yet public accusation would collapse both reputations.

The party is enlisted to act as invisible auditors. Their task: breach the vault under cover of night, confirm the taint using Ilexi’s Reagent, and return without leaving political fingerprints.

Mission Briefing

  • Employer: High Broker Mavin Relth of the Goldmark Exchange
  • Objective: Infiltrate Vault 7B beneath Trennel Holdings and test 2–3 silver bars using Ilexi’s Reagent
  • Avoid: Detection, damage to the vault, confrontation with Trennel guards
  • Reward: 300 gp + one uncommon alchemical item per character (delivered later)

Vault Details

Vault 7B is warded by:

  • Mechanical lock (DC 18 Disable Device)
  • Magical glyph alarm (DC 17 Spellcraft to identify, DC 20 Dispel or careful bypass)
  • 1–2 rotating night guards above, plus a courtyard that must be crossed to reach the service stairs

The vault is located in a sub-basement below the Trennel Counting House, accessed via a side cellar door near the loading dock.

Encounter Flow

Phase I – Reconnaissance

  • Players may stake out Trennel’s estate for 1–2 days.
  • DC 15 Perception reveals guard patrol patterns.
  • Optional: Contact with a local sewer guide offers an underground route (with its own hazards).

Phase II – Infiltration

  • Entry via rooftop or loading stairs leads into lightly trapped corridors.
  • Locked vault requires stealth, Disable Device, and Spellcraft checks.
  • Guards may arrive mid-check (DC 12 Stealth per round to avoid).

Phase III – Confirmation

  • Inside the vault, several crates are marked for “neutral export.”
  • At least 2 bars inside one crate are Hollow Chill tainted (verified by Ilexi’s Reagent).
  • The reagent glows green. If applied to more than 3 samples, a bar may crumble slightly and activate a minor alarm.

Phase IV – Escape

  • Escape is complicated by one patrolling elite house guard (CR 2).
  • Optional: PCs may forge a new crate seal to hide evidence of entry (DC 16 Forgery or Craft).
