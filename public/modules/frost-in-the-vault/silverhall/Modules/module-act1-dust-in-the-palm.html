Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Act I

Act I: Dust in the Palm

Level 1 Adventure – The fracture begins in stillness.

Overview

Silverhall thrives in outward elegance but inward decay. Within House Lebeda’s coin halls, a single coin disintegrates during a routine audit, triggering a quiet alarm at the highest level. An investigation begins not with a warband, but with six unlikely allies summoned under secret wax. The party arrives as strangers — and departs as a unit forged in uncertainty.

Key Objectives

  • Introduce the six party members with interwoven motivations.
  • Investigate the first instance of Hollow Chill contamination.
  • Explore Silverhall, its factions, forges, and hidden tensions.
  • Uncover multiple coin samples infected by the Hollow Chill.
  • Engage in the first skirmish — a silent theft attempt in the mints.

Scene 1 – The Crumbling Coin

The party is brought separately to the vaults below House Lebeda’s shrine of Abadar. An alchemist demonstrates the reagent. A coin crumbles in their presence. Lady Elyra Lebeda delivers the hook with veiled tension:

Lady Elyra’s Speech:
“You are not here as swords. You are here as shadows. Something is wrong beneath our wealth — and I need answers before our enemies need weapons.”

Allow the players to inspect the vault, use skill checks (Knowledge [Arcana], Alchemy, Sense Motive) and establish their roles.

Scene 2 – City Threads

The party is given quarters in a neutral mercantile inn and tasked with discrete investigation. During this segment, they may:

  • Question bankers, mint masters, or rival guilds.
  • Track the coin’s ledger back to a closed forge.
  • Notice several similar coins already in circulation — one with recent, accelerated decay.

Skill checks should lead them toward the first hidden stash of tainted coin: a bribe paid to a dock official, now breaking apart.

Scene 3 – Vault of Echoes

The final encounter of Act I is a stealthy break-in at the decommissioned eastern forge. The party finds it briefly reactivated, with foreign tools and strange alchemical residue near the vents.

Inside, they discover a pair of intruders attempting to burn documents — agents working on behalf of a hidden intermediary.

Combat Encounter (CR 2):
2x Skilled Rogues with alchemical torches and smoke bombs.
Use terrain and vents to make the fight disorienting.

Rewards

  • Discovery of early Hollow Chill sample (+ XP, reagent vials)
  • First map clue leading toward deeper corruption (Act II)
  • +1 Reputation with House Lebeda (if handled discretely)
