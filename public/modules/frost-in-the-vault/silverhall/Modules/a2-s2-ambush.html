Act 2, Scene 2: Ash and Chain

Recommended Level: 2
Location: Glenebon Uplands, half-day march from Silverhall
Overview: The party investigates a Coldheart-related shipment source, discovering a hobgoblin/bugbear outpost tied to illegal ore delivery from the Branthlaend Mountains.

Scene Structure

Subscene 1: Arrival at the Uplands

Guided by a coded mark on a crumpled parchment from Act 1, the party treks into the high Glenebon ridges. They spot goblinoid tracks, overhead glider hooks, and a worn camp trail.

Subscene 2: Observation and Tactical Planning

The party discovers a small encampment of Coldheart-linked goblinoids. A reconnaissance opportunity allows the players to witness a daily patrol leaving, giving them a window to lay traps or sneak in.

Subscene 3: The Ambush at Ravine Pass

The returning patrol stumbles into a deadly prepared ambush if the party sets traps. The terrain includes a tripwire, entangle spell zone, and a pressure-plate alchemical fire trap. Combat opens with a surprise round.

Pre-Set Traps & Wards

  • Ilexi's Sticky Inferno: Pressure trap. Alchemist's Fire + Grease effect. Reflex DC 15, 1d6 fire, splash, then Grease (DC 11 or fall).
  • Sava's Tripwire: Reflex DC 13 or fall prone and entangled in brush.
  • Karzak's Entangle Ward: 20-ft radius spell. DC 14 Reflex or entangled.

Combat Encounter: Round 1 Summary

All PCs act in a surprise round. Enemies are flat-footed.

Ilexi → Throws Tanglefoot Bomb at Sergeant (Hit, entangled & movement zero)
Sava  → Triggers Tripwire (Trooper A falls), shoots (7 dmg → KO)
Velra → Casts Bane (3 enemies fail, -1 to hit/saves)
Karzak → Activates Entangle (Sergeant & 1 trooper entangled)
Fosk  → Charges Trooper B (6 dmg, drops to 0 HP)
Serune → Charges in, activates Smite Evil (targets Grask)
Lazlo → Casts Color Spray (Sergeant fails → blind & stunned, Trooper C unconscious)

Aftermath Options

  • If party wins: They find a Coldheart manifest showing planned shipment to "Vaers Hollow" – leading into Act 2, Scene 3.
  • If captured: Grask interrogates PCs. Escape option mid-night when goblins are drunk and careless.
  • If stealth: Discover weapon cache and ore vault. Clues to Branthlaend Mountain extraction ops.

Design Notes

  • This is a Deadly CR-balanced fight if the party doesn’t ambush.
  • Encourages creativity via traps, terrain use, and flanking.
  • Pairs well with Fosk and Ilexi’s skill strengths.
