Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Prologue

Prologue: Cracks in the Coin

A Kingdom on the Edge

In the northern reaches of Avistan, where Brevoy’s brittle alliance of noble houses holds the realm together like frost-laced mortar, a slow rot has taken root — one so subtle it cannot be seen, only felt.

Tensions between the Dragonmarked Houses of Issia and Rostland, long held at bay by the unsteady balance of political marriages, mercantile bribery, and militarized diplomacy, are once again poised to erupt. At the heart of the matter lies House Surtova, the current ruling power of Brevoy. Their cold grip from New Stetven to Port Ice has always been tight — but recently, it has grown precise.

Their most dangerous weapon? Not blade nor spell — but money.

The Hollow Chill

Unknown to the public, House Surtova has funded arcane-alchemical research through intermediaries in Port Ice and Highdelve, unearthing a forbidden technique known only as the Hollow Chill. This ancient process allows metal to be tainted at the point of forging, altering its very nature with a cold-born magical agent.

The result is nearly indistinguishable from true coinage — until the heat of use or time itself causes it to collapse into shimmering dust.

House Orlovsky’s Quiet Desperation

In the city of Grayhaven, ancestral seat of House Orlovsky, alarm has begun to spread behind shuttered windows. Coin hoards meant to secure grain shipments, bribe mercenaries, or reinforce the walls of the Golushkin range have begun to degrade. Bankers swear loyalty even as they pad their vaults with iron. Generals admit what no soldier dares voice aloud: “We can no longer pay our armies with confidence.”

Silverhall: The First Fracture

The tale begins not in a battlefield, but in a ledger. In Silverhall, seat of House Lebeda, a routine audit uncovers the first undeniable proof of the Hollow Chill’s reach. A shipment of gold, sealed by Abadaran priests and untouched in a noble vault, collapses under inspection — flaking into gleaming motes and deathly silence.

A young alchemist, Ilexi Tinctwhistle, develops a reagent that reacts to the tainted metal. House Lebeda convenes a quiet alliance with select allies. They send six summons.

The Six

They are not generals. Not saints. Not spies. But they are skilled. And they are not aligned with House Surtova.

  • Velra Wynne – Tiefling Inquisitor of Abadar, a judge once disavowed for showing mercy.
  • Karzak Deepstem – Dwarven Cave Druid, survivor of a buried truth.
  • Lazlo Oerlen – Frost-marked Magus, touched by something he does not yet understand.
  • Ilexi Tinctwhistle – Gnome chemist who found a truth she was not meant to uncover.
  • Sava of Zmeyka – Half-elf courier and blade, carrying secrets from both sides of the river.
  • Serune Quen – Dhampir Armiger, traitor to House Surtova — and proud of it.

Together, they are not a warband, nor a unit. They are a question Brevoy has not yet answered: “What happens when justice begins with a crumbling coin?”
