Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Act II Encounter Extension

A2-4B: The Delivery – Coin for Loyalty

A dangerous chance to intercept a Hollow Chill delivery — or become part of the machine.

Background

Following the events of A2-4: Silver for Swords, the party discovers a half-burned note hidden among Albric Dannemoor’s belongings. It mentions a specific date and a transfer point along a remote route where a new shipment of Hollow Chill coin will be delivered.

The shipment — disguised as grain or ore — is actually stored in magically shielded strongboxes on a large covered wagon. It’s accompanied by several armed intermediaries who expect to hand it off to a representative of Albric’s trade group and collect their 10% fee in untainted coin.

Encounter Setup

  • Location: Forked trail clearing between the Gray River ferry road and the Ridgewood camp.
  • Objective: Intercept or impersonate the receiver to secure the shipment.
  • Complication: The delivery crew is suspicious, armed, and magically tested against deceit.

Options for the Party

  • Ambush: Attack the guards and delivery crew directly before the transfer.
  • Deception: Disguise themselves as Albric’s agents and bluff their way through the handoff.
  • Disruption: Trail the delivery and attempt to sabotage or divert it en route.

Delivery Crew Composition

  • 1x Surtova Field Negotiator (CR 2 Bard or Sorcerer, uses Suggestion and Detect Thoughts)
  • 3x Wagon Guards (CR 1 Warriors with crossbows and glaives)
  • 2x Pack Beasts (Enchanted oxen, non-combat unless provoked)

Expected Transaction

Delivery ComponentValueExpected Fee
Sealed coin chest (Hollow Chill)500 gp50 gp in real coin
Ledger with trade routing orders—Returned post-verification

Any deviation from expected names, timing, or behavior prompts suspicion (Sense Motive +6). A disguised party must pass a DC 17 Bluff or DC 15 Disguise check — with advantage if they recovered Albric’s merchant seal or phrase token.

Post-Encounter Outcomes

  • If the party seizes the coin, they may return it to House Lebeda or use it as bait.
  • If impersonation fails, the negotiator may attempt to flee with a Sending stone warning.
  • If successful, they gain the shipment and possibly a forged connection to future deliveries.
GM Tip:
Let this be a moment of moral conflict — the players are now in control of counterfeit magical currency. What they do next could change the shape of the war between the houses.
