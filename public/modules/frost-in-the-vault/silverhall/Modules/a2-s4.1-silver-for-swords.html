Frost in the Vaults: Silverhall – Act II Encounter

A2-4: Silver for Swords

Economic sabotage, moral compromise, and the true cost of corrupted trade.

Overview

Silverhall’s economy, once thriving, begins to show cracks at the 12–15 month mark of Hollow Chill introduction. Coins that once passed without question now flake under pressure. Traders whisper. Vault keepers panic. And hidden truths claw to the surface.

This encounter places the party in the midst of discovery, coercion, and hard choices as they uncover a quiet conspiracy within the merchant network.

The Trader’s Trick

Albric Dannemoor, a respected grain dealer, has used a forest path detour for over a year to swap legitimate coin for Hollow Chill-tainted bars. The swap site is a roadside cache along the Gray River path, unknown until now.

A caravaneer’s confession sparks the investigation. House Lebeda discreetly hires the party to confirm or deny the swap.

Leads and Hooks

  • Intercepted coin shipment found to be underweight and structurally compromised.
  • Vault clerks report crumbling coins at the bottom of tightly packed chests.
  • A priest passes on a confession from a terrified caravan worker.

Key Phases

1. Surveillance

  • DC 15 Survival or DC 17 Society to detect Albric’s detour from the main road.
  • Tracks lead to a camouflaged cache near a marked boulder.

2. The Swap Site

As the party arrives:

  • 1x Surtova Intermediary (CR 2 Alchemist or Bard)
  • 2x Roomstomper Bodyguards (CR 1 Fighters)
  • Albric Dannemoor (CR 1/2 Expert, non-combatant)

A locked chest holds tainted coin. With Ilexi’s Reagent, it fluoresces green.

3. Resolution Paths

  • Confront Albric and extract a confession or seize his records.
  • Expose or protect the trader — possibly recruit him as a double agent.
  • Attempt to follow the trail upstream toward larger Surtova operations.

GM Tip:
Let this encounter serve as a pressure point — a chance for the party to define their values. Do they protect the trader? Expose the system? Bargain for leverage? This is where faction alignment and reputation can start to matter.
